# Crypto bull run winners

Data as of 24 Oct 2026. Prices: CoinGecko (daily USD). No social-media sources.
**Not financial advice.** 2021 figures are partly unverified (see that section). Research reference only; no MTS layer or trading logic depends on it.

## Short version

- **Bitcoin's bounce:** daily low $58.6k on 1 Jul 2026, stayed roughly $58-66k (daily closes) until it broke out around 20 Aug; now about $81k (+38% from the low). Solana is +38% over the same window, so it is a fair yardstick.
- **Since the bounce, 64 of 199 coins in the top 200 by market cap (stablecoins and tokenized assets included) beat Solana.** The top 4 gainers (Akedo, Bitway, Quack AI, Pearl) are young tokens (Pearl and Bitway listed in 2026; Akedo and Quack AI under a year old, from tiny base prices). Treat as lottery tickets: launch pumps, thin float, and Bitway barely trades.
- **Winners with substance:** Zcash, NEAR (AI angle), Uniswap, Raydium, Quant. Big names with a "backer" story lagged: Hedera +10%, Render +12%, Bittensor +23%, Fetch.ai +2%.
- **2021:** the coins that out-ran Solana (Shiba, Gala, Axie, Sandbox, PancakeSwap, Yearn) are now 94-100% below their peaks. Hedera's 2021 run was +5,700%, well below Solana's. Early-cycle launches did the biggest percentages, and most were the biggest losers after.
- **Action:** If you chase the new tokens, size small and check real daily volume first. For core exposure, the "real" rows below are the ones with years of history and liquidity. Not financial advice.
