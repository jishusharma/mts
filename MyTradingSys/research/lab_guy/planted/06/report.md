## Evidence interpretation

`MATCH` means the particular assertion is supported by the identified source, not that the trade is fully replayable. `MISMATCH` means a positive assertion conflicts with the cited source or its actual contents. `NOT_IN_DATA` means the evidence needed to decide is absent, obscured or ambiguous. A blank outcome is not a loss or a zero return.

Separate three evidence types: **frame-observed** position/order/annotation; **quoted** trader statement (here often an AI-translated English caption, whose translation accuracy was not independently checked); **inferred** relationship between observations. An order label is not a fill report. A displayed average entry may be rounded. An SL label is a resting order, not proof of execution at that price. A taskbar clock dates the recording; it does not automatically date a pasted screenshot, historical candle, or entry. A chart cursor dates a selected bar, not necessarily the trade. Timezone is not established merely by a German desktop.

The row table checks side and quantity together with displayed entry (`E`), stop (`SL`), and target (`TP`). Missing evidence requests name what is required, not an assertion that the requested frame exists. All numerical levels are transcribed observations, not proposed strategy parameters.
