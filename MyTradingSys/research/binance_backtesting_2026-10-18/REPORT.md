# Reliable backtesting: Binance BTC and the general research standard

Date: 2026-10-18. Scope: evidence review and implementation roadmap, not a profitability experiment or permission to trade. Repository inspected at `cb007264` (origin/fast-track). No sealed market outcomes were read. External sources were opened and checked on 2026-10-18; access limitations are explicit below. Independent Sol 6.1 review is recorded in REVIEW.md.

## Decision

Backtesting is a conditional experiment: what a precisely specified strategy would have done on specified historical information under specified execution assumptions. A profitable chart is insufficient evidence. MTS should require reproducible data, causal signals, realistic cash accounting, honest search accounting, adequately powered out-of-sample tests, and execution validation before interpreting a result as deployable evidence.

These are recommended mandatory research safeguards, not claims that every strategy needs the same statistical test. PBO, DSR and a chronological holdout answer different questions. No paper establishes a universal minimum trade count, optimal BTC indicator, safe leverage, fee rate, or guaranteed profitable backtest procedure.

The most directly relevant empirical warning is Hudson and Urquhart: their broad technical-rule study finds positive results within its historical sample but no Bitcoin predictability in its out-of-sample period [S6]. This does not prove all BTC strategies fail. A recent perpetual-futures preprint shows that more elaborate screening still leaves material selection risk [S9]. Our conclusion is to strengthen the experiment before expanding the strategy search.

## 1. Research the research method first

### Method note, specified before the source search

Evidence must distinguish selection bias, information leakage and execution error from genuine loss of economic predictability. Assess methods using known-truth simulations and empirical studies with stated instruments, horizons, samples, time-ordered out-of-sample tests and fees/funding/slippage; verify Binance mechanics independently against exchange documentation. External evidence ranks safeguards and hypotheses; only an MTS experiment can support an MTS edge claim.

### Methodology research and its application

Morris, White and Crowther recommend ADEMP: aims, data-generating mechanisms, estimands, methods and performance measures, with simulation uncertainty [S1]. Adaptation here: define the question and target outcome first; distinguish actual market replay from synthetic tests of the simulator; specify what constitutes a false pass, false rejection or inconclusive result. Their work is methodology, not evidence of crypto returns.

This is a targeted critical review, not an exhaustive systematic review or meta-analysis. Searches covered backtest overfitting/PBO, deflated Sharpe, data snooping, serial dependence, technical trading OOS in crypto, market fragmentation, wash trading, perpetual execution, and Binance archive/funding/order specifications. Foundational papers were searched without a date floor; crypto and exchange claims were checked through the research date. Earlier MTS reviews supplied leads, not verified facts. The evidence includes favorable and unfavorable results, not only negative studies.

Admission rules: open the primary paper, publisher/author repository, or official exchange documentation; match author/title/version/date and the exact supported claim. Full text is preferable; abstract-only claims remain limited to the abstract. Apply the existing playbook tiers: T1 peer-reviewed or institutional research; T2 disclosed preprints and exchange documentation; T3 vendor implementation guidance. Confidence is confidence in the stated finding, not in transferable profitability. A search snippet is not verification.

Extraction fields: source ID; version; sample/instrument/horizon; method; OOS and cost coverage; outcome; limitation; proposed control. Proposed engineering choices are explicitly OURS. No quantitative result from another study is transplanted into a strategy parameter.

## 3. Why backtests fail, and the mandatory controls

The following controls are OURS: engineering/research requirements synthesized from the evidence. They are not numerical rules claimed by a trader or universal thresholds mandated by the papers.

| Failure mechanism | Mandatory control and observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Repeatedly selecting the lucky winner; hidden manual/chart/LLM experiments | Append-only trial and data-access ledger, including abandoned variants, cost/exit/regime changes and inspected outcome periods. Identify the hypothesis family and adjustment method before evaluating it. Preserve complete aligned candidate returns where family tests require them. [S2-S4] |
| Tuning on validation or repeatedly peeking at holdout | Freeze code/config/data hashes and decision rule before final evaluation. Inner selection uses earlier data; chronological outer evaluation uses later data. Once seen, a holdout is consumed; changes require new future evidence. PBO is complementary, not permission to reuse it. [S2-S4, S6; OURS temporal design] |
| Future information hidden inside features | For every field record event time and earliest availability time. Truncate input at time t, recompute, and require historical signals to stay unchanged. Completed higher-timeframe bars only; pivots become available at confirmation; normalization/model fitting uses training data. Purge training labels whose outcome intervals overlap evaluation and derive any embargo from label/feature information spans, not an arbitrary percentage. |
| Wrong observation unit and underestimated uncertainty | Use a fixed-frequency marked-to-market portfolio equity series, including idle periods and simultaneous exposure. Use block/cluster-aware uncertainty when trades overlap or cluster; disclose block choice and sensitivity. IID resampling of trades is not automatically valid. [S5; OURS implementation] |
| Low power mistaken for proof of no edge | Predeclare economically worthwhile net effect and estimate power/MDE under dependence and plausible costs. A CI covering both zero and useful gains is INCONCLUSIVE. A precise upper bound below the useful edge is evidence against that target. Trade count alone cannot decide. [S1, S3, S5; OURS decision policy] |
| Gross profits consumed by turnover | Charge entry/exit fees on actual fill notional and fee currency; add spread, latency slippage, adverse selection, impact/capacity and funding/borrow where applicable. Report gross-to-net reconciliation and break-even cost. Avoid double-counting spread already included in bid/ask fills. [B2-B7, S9, V1] |
| Impossible fills or path selection | A signal computed from bar close cannot fill earlier within that bar. Resolve stop/target order with finer data; otherwise report both possible paths or a declared conservative scenario. Touch is not queue execution; trade-through is a sensitivity scenario, not guaranteed truth or a mathematical PnL bound. Model partial fills, cancellation latency, rejects, gaps and no-fill cases. [S11, V1] |
| Survivorship and convenient regimes | For multi-coin work use the contemporaneous universe, delisted assets and availability dates. BTC-only work still has venue/date selection. Freeze regime definitions using contemporaneously available information and show pooled plus regime results without selecting a winning subgroup afterward. [S6-S8; OURS design] |
| Bull-market exposure mistaken for skill | Compare against no-trade and fee-consistent buy-and-hold, and an exposure/risk-matched comparator where feasible. Random-entry controls must share session, holding/exposure constraints, order mechanics and costs. Benchmark search is also a research choice. [S4, S6; OURS implementation] |
| Simulator correct, strategy economically dead | Diagnose gross effect, turnover, fee margin, drawdowns and regime concentration separately. Do not repair a rejected result by changing stops/timeframes and keeping the original trial identity. A correct negative result is useful. |
| Backtest/testnet-to-market gap | Replay signals and accounting identically in research and shadow operation; use public production-market observations to calibrate execution assumptions without placing production orders. Testnet validates integration, not production capacity. Shadow fills still cannot prove actual queue position or own-order impact. [S11, V1; OURS scope] |

## 4. Binance BTC specification that must accompany every run

**Product identity:** select spot BTCUSDT, USD-M linear perpetual BTCUSDT, or a specific COIN-M/inverse/dated contract. Record venue, symbol, settlement/collateral currency, trading availability and contract version. A spot OHLCV test is not a perpetual test. Shorting spot needs an explicit borrowing model; inverse contracts need coin-denominated accounting. Cross-venue Binance signals/Deribit execution are a separate transfer experiment.

**Data contract:** retain retrieval URL/time, raw checksum, normalized-file hash, schema and timestamp unit, coverage, duplicates/gaps and correction history. Verify the 2024/2025 spot boundary explicitly [B1]. No silent forward-filled trade/volume bars; absent funding/OI is missing, not zero. Use UTC event time and explicit local-session/DST conversion if the strategy needs it. Downloaded historical data may be revised: record this retrospective limitation when original-as-seen data is unavailable.

**Granularity:** candles support bar-level hypotheses, but cannot recover event order, queue position or hidden liquidity. AggTrades support aggressor-side traded flow, not resting-book imbalance [B6]. Depth snapshots/bands are not a complete order-event history. A request needing absent data gets NOT_IN_DATA or a scoped sensitivity analysis, not fabricated precision.

**Funding:** debit/credit only at applicable settlement events while the position is eligible; simulate changing size, signed rate and event mark value. Historical realized funding may determine the later cashflow but cannot be exposed to an earlier signal. Settlement on the same timestamp as an entry/exit requires an explicit event ordering; sensitivity-test unresolved ties. Do not hardcode eight-hour payments for all symbols and dates [B2-B3].

**Fees and fills:** effective-dated maker/taker tier, discounts/promotions and fee asset; order type alone does not prove maker status. If historical fee eligibility cannot be established, label the assumed scenario and stress it. Use the appropriate bid/ask side, order increments and notional/quantity restrictions [B5-B7]. Report zero-cost, fee-only and fully costed scenarios as diagnostics; only a justified fully costed scenario can support a net-edge claim.

**Leverage:** maintain wallet, collateral, unrealized PnL, margin requirements and equity through time; apply actual product maintenance tiers and mark-price liquidation [B4]. Leverage is not simply multiplying an unlevered return stream. Stress exchange outages, delayed exits, liquidation gaps and stablecoin/collateral conversion as scenarios with declared assumptions. No claim that a scenario's probability has been measured here.

**Metrics:** fixed-frequency net equity returns, benchmark-relative performance, drawdown depth/duration, exposure, turnover, fill/no-fill counts, fee/funding/implementation-shortfall attribution, concentration, uncertainty, MDE and search-adjusted evidence. Record annualization convention (crypto trades continuously), leverage and return denominator. Win rate and profit factor alone cannot establish an edge.

## 7. Verification limits and discarded leads

- No new BTC profitability backtest, historical fee reconstruction, full Binance order-book reconstruction, or independent replication of a paper was performed. Model review checks reasoning; it is not empirical replication or proof of future returns.
- Hudson publisher/PDF direct access failed; institutional abstract was opened instead. Claims were limited accordingly.
- Cong NBER/PDF and an attempted unrelated publisher route were inaccessible; the opened author preprint is used at T2. No venue-specific wash-trading allegation is inferred.
- Third-party PBO calculators, Reddit, marketing and search-only performance summaries were excluded as foundations.
- AutoQuant publication status was not confirmed by a publisher; keep T2 despite its manuscript journal label. Its parameters, funding fallback and returns are not adopted.
- No validated evidence found in this review for a universal profitable Binance BTC strategy, universal minimum N, or a fixed slippage/fee value valid across products and time.
