## Rules

### R1. Volume never in isolation (SOURCED)

Rule: Judge volume against the same time of day on prior days; falling volume means fewer large participants active.

> "whenever trading volumes decrease, supply and demand are no longer as high. This means that fewer large market participants are interested in being active in the markets at that time."
**Source GPS: YkclL6xgu-s | BEGIN**

### R2. Open volume trend = inflow, not direction (SOURCED)

Rule: Rising or falling first-5-minute volume says whether liquidity must be placed, not whether price goes up or down.

> "But I still don't know whether the markets will rise or fall as a result."
**Source GPS: YkclL6xgu-s | BEGIN**

### R3. Who is forced: closing-auction block orders (SOURCED)

Rule: Large institutions finish unplaced orders in the closing auction where block counterparties exist (index-futures session clock; not a BTC rule).

> "you'll bring the remaining shares to the market during the closing auction because it's precisely in the closing auctions that you often find trading partners who want to buy or sell in large blocks."
**Source GPS: YkclL6xgu-s | MIDDLE**

### R4. Large players need balance (SOURCED)

Rule: Large participants need sideways phases to position at fair prices and dislike high volatility.

> "Large market participants need time to secure fair prices in the market. They really don't like it when the markets are extremely volatile."
**Source GPS: YkclL6xgu-s | MIDDLE**

### R5. Thin close pulls price back to POC (SOURCED)

Rule: A weekly close on little volume below value, with the volume POC above, makes a return to equilibrium more likely than continued fall.

> "there was little trading volume. And the logical consequence from this since we also have the point of control that is the volume weighted center of gravity up here the market moved back up again the following week."
**Source GPS: YkclL6xgu-s | MIDDLE**

### R6. Acceptance below big volume (SOURCED)

Rule: Real volume trading below the large-volume zone means lower prices are accepted and the downtrend likely continues; same upward.

> "Then the market is simply doing nothing other than accepting lower prices. And greater acceptance of lower prices means that for now the probability is high that the downtrend will continue."
**Source GPS: YkclL6xgu-s | END**

### R7. Stop and target from volume (SOURCED)

Rule: Stop below the last volume; target at prior large-volume areas.

> "where does my stop loss belong. Yes, below the last volumes. And where are my targets? with high probability there where there used to be large volumes in the past."
**Source GPS: YkclL6xgu-s | END**

### R8. Per-instrument size (SOURCED)

Rule: What is a large volume is instrument specific.

> "know which volume sizes are typical for each instrument."
**Source GPS: YkclL6xgu-s | END**
