## Rules

### R1. Spot the algo in the book (SOURCED)

Rule: HFT and market-making footprints show in the order book or ladder, for example absorption versus plain movement.

> "certain types you can recognize quite well. For example, by looking at the order book or the price ladder, what they're thinking or what strategy they're currently using."
**Source GPS: ACb0Qtt3DSQ | BEGIN**

### R2. Algo malfunction (SOURCED)

Rule: When an algorithm misfires, order flow or the book shows it and one can trade the dislocation.

> "It's possible that something goes wrong in the markets and we can spot that for example through the order flow or the order book."
**Source GPS: ACb0Qtt3DSQ | BEGIN**

### R3. Accumulation of liquidity (SOURCED)

Rule: A visible large accumulation of liquidity can be used for a trade in the moment.

> "there are situations where we see someone accumulating a large amount of liquidity and we can recognize that and use it for ourselves in the moment to trade."
**Source GPS: ACb0Qtt3DSQ | BEGIN**

### R4. Congestion then reversal (SOURCED)

Rule: Price pushed fast through a clear road slows when congestion forms (absorption); a counter move or correction follows.

> "When the market starts to slow down and in a way congestion forms for example through absorption the market can first move back in a counter move a reversal can form or sometimes a correction"
**Source GPS: ACb0Qtt3DSQ | BEGIN**

### R5. Clear road = breakout (SOURCED)

Rule: With no resistance in the book, price is pushed strongly one way; breakouts follow.

> "When the market is a bit clear, meaning there's no resistance, the price gets pushed very strongly and very quickly in one direction."
**Source GPS: ACb0Qtt3DSQ | BEGIN**

### R6. Crypto front-running (SOURCED)

Rule: In crypto a real large resting order plus a slowdown lets one position ahead of it; a market buy moves thin crypto books easily.

> "you can do front running there and it works really well if you see a large order um liquidity in the book and it turns out to be real."
**Source GPS: ACb0Qtt3DSQ | MIDDLE**

### R7. Track speed and absorbed liquidity (SOURCED)

Rule: Most common scalp: track tape speed and where liquidity is absorbed, then trade with or against it.

> "the most common strategy is to track the speed and in a way see where the liquidity is being absorbed, so to speak."
**Source GPS: ACb0Qtt3DSQ | MIDDLE**

### R8. Order flow only at levels (SOURCED)

Rule: Use order-flow methods only at pre-selected price levels, as confirmation of a breakout idea.

> "simply use the methods you use in order flow scalping only at price levels, for example, where it's interesting."
**Source GPS: ACb0Qtt3DSQ | END**
