## Rules

### R1. Context before counter-trade (SOURCED)

Rule: Without context there are thousands of counter-trade examples and many fail; context says where to look.

> "if you have no context if you have no idea where you can look for it you found thousands of ways and uh examples it works but a lot of them don't works"
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | BEGIN**

### R2. Volume and price first, order flow for timing (SOURCED)

Rule: Volume and price are enough for most of the edge; order flow adds the moment of entry and better timing.

> "volume and price is everything I need but the order flow give me the moment"
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | MIDDLE**

### R3. Footprint for the local entry point (SOURCED)

Rule: Footprint charts find the best local price to get in or out, especially with limit orders.

> "also footprint charts are working very good to look locally the where's the best place to go inside or outside if you trade with limit orders or anything else."
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | MIDDLE**

### R4. Few setups, read speed (SOURCED)

Rule: Four or five order-flow setups are enough; most of the reading is speed with simple things.

> "I only take four or five. This is enough for me. I can read everything with speed with the sing simple things."
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | MIDDLE**

### R5. Speed at a breakout: where to watch (SOURCED)

Rule: Speed shows interest or no interest in a breakout, but one must know where to watch for it; it cannot be localised from a candlestick chart alone.

> "You must know where's the place to looking for a nice breakout."
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | MIDDLE**

### R6. Bear markets move fast both ways (SOURCED)

Rule: In a real bear market price goes fast down and fast up repeatedly, not just down; trade both with knowledge of the mechanics (opinion, no data shown).

> "bearish market is in the bearish market is it goes fast down and fast up and fast down and fast up."
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | MIDDLE**

### R7. Volatility present but big money absent (SOURCED)

Rule: In a squeeze-type event the book shows volatility with the big money gone; wait for that point, then size in against the move (stock example: GameStop, not a BTC rule).

> "you can see the volatility is present but the big money is out."
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | MIDDLE**

### R8. Fair versus unfair price decides the style (SOURCED)

Rule: Volume profile tells fair and unfair price: at unfair levels look for fast trades or counter-trades; in value, scalp.

> "when the market is in unfair unfair price regions levels areas you can look for fast trades or for counter trades and if the price is uh in value you can make all the time scalping"
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | END**

### R9. A setup is good only at a place for a trader type (SOURCED)

Rule: No setup is good in itself; it is good for a given trader type at a given location.

> "This setup is good for a counter trader on an interesting location."
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | END**

### R10. Drawdown cap (SOURCED)

Rule: Plan so the drawdown never exceeds 15 to 20 percent; beyond that it is not real trading.

> "be true what is able for you and have a good vision and a good uh plan to prepare that you never got a draw down higher than 15 to 20%."
**Source GPS: AtR_c-B0jw8 | END**
