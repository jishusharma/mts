## Summary (6 lines, plain words, OURS)

1. **5m, 15m, 1h, 4h: a DI cross with ADX>20 does not beat just holding, after the 4 bps fee.** On 5m the long cross is significantly worse than baseline (t -3 to -4). Only one intraday cell stands out: 15m "ADX rises through 25 with +DI above -DI" (n=1837, +0.06% vs -0.02% baseline at 12 bars, t 3.2). One cell out of ~48 is what chance alone can give; not a finding.
2. **1d is the only frame where the 25-cross shows a measurable help, and it works in BOTH directions** (n=34 longs: +2.9% / +4.1% / +5.6% at 3/6/12 days vs +0.6 / +1.1 / +2.3 baseline, win 65-74%; n=33 shorts: +1.9% / +3.2% / +4.7% vs baseline -0.6 / -1.1 / -2.3, t up to 3.0). Read it as "a trend is starting" more than a direction call; ~34 events over 9.8 years is thin.
3. **The CCU-style 2d cross is not supported here:** 2d +DI-over--DI cross with ADX>20 (n=33) is below baseline (-2.4% vs +1.1% at 3 bars, t -1.8); the 2d 25-cross has n=17 and n=20 and they disagree (long +, short -): UNDERPOWERED. 5d and 1w: every cell has n<30, so the data cannot say anything (UNKNOWN).
4. **ADX<20 is 19-35% of bars (35% on 5m, 30% on 1h, 24% on 1d, 19% on 1w), but fading it does not pay:** fade-last-bar and fade-the-DI-leader earn about zero before fees on 5m-4h (net about -0.04% = the fee) and lose on 1d+ (-0.3% to -10%); following the DI leader is positive on 1d+ in BOTH ADX states, so low ADX does not flip BTC into mean reversion on these rules.
5. **ADX peak above 40 then first turn down is not "trend over":** ADX is still above 25 twelve bars later in 92-100% of cases, and on 1h/4h the move after the peak is larger than average (4h: |3.6%| vs 2.9%) with the trend direction winning 57% at 12 bars (n=219, +0.9% net). Use a peak as "stretched, volatile", not as a reversal signal.
6. **Now:** 1h and 4h show ADX 54 / 53 with -DI far above +DI (a strong, mature down move); 1d/2d/5d/1w all sit at ADX 22-25 with -DI above +DI, i.e. no strong higher-frame trend yet. Caveats: one asset, one 6.6-year intraday sample (2020-04 onward, mostly one bull-bear cycle), overlapping windows, ~150 cells so some t>2 are expected by chance, Bybit prices not Binance.
