## Backtest: yes, it makes sense if the pivot is defined causally (card oi-pivot-reversal-probability-by-tf)
1. Two objects, kept separate. A pivot LABEL (the swing high/low you see afterwards) is lookahead and is only the outcome. A pivot CANDIDATE is what the bar shows at its close: OI slope flip after an N-bar decline, price-vs-OI divergence state, taker-delta sign, volume spike, location vs levels known at that time.
2. Swing labels: the repo already has the rule (CYCLE_DESIGN.md, candle_close_2026-10-14: five-bar pivot, usable only at j+2 close, ties are not pivots). Reuse it per TF (5m, 15m, 1h, 4h).
3. Question per TF: P(reversal of >= k x ATR before continuation of k x ATR, within H bars | candidate features, context) against the base rate of all bars at the same location. Report hits, misses, timeouts, censored separately; confirmation delay and entry-to-extreme distance mandatory (CYCLE_DESIGN rules).
4. Context columns from card context-conditioned-session-flow-states (HTF balance vs trend, range position, weekday/weekend, vol percentile, OI trend). The operator's claim is testable: the same candidate should have a stable reversal rate inside a context and a different one across contexts.
5. All thresholds (N, k, H, spike size) are OURS, chosen on DEV, checked once on VALIDATION.
6. What already exists: OI_DIVERGENCE_2026-10-10 / OI_EVENT_STUDY_2026-10-10 tested 1h price-vs-OI divergence over 28 days without context or location: no edge (t <= 1.22).
