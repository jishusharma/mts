### C3. Absorption divergence (SOURCED)

Rule: price does not break the previous low (or high) while delta/CVD does break it: the aggressive side made the effort and was absorbed. Reversal signal on the absorbing side.

> "So this type of divergence where price can't break but delta breaks." [0:06:15]
**Source GPS: Ni6quY00dcw | MIDDLE**

> "So we have this divergence is called an absorption divergence." [0:06:30]

### C4. Exhaustion (lack of participation) divergence (SOURCED)

Rule: price makes a new low (high) but CVD does not: little participation in the push, exhaustion of that side, reversal signal.

> "So when price goes low even breaks the previous low but the CVD doesn't it means that in this impulse there is less participation less interest and in general a lack of participation." [0:08:23]
