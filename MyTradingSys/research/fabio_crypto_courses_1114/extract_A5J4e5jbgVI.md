### A8. Strong low, retracement and failed auctions above highs (SOURCED)

Rule: a breakdown with big volume that is matched by big volume on the way up before it = a "strong low" (support holds). A single sell candle that breaks out and leaves a fair value gap is probably only a retracement, not a new downtrend. Above prior highs, failed auctions are common; the breakout candle with 8,000 contracts and big trades looked like continuation, not a failed auction. Responsive sellers are expected near the value area high and the highest volume node. No numbers beyond these examples.
