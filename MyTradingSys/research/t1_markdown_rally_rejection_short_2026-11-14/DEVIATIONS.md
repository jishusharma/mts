# DEVIATIONS and implementation readings (t1, DEV build 2026-11-14)

PRE_REG.md is untouched. Nothing below changes a frozen parameter; items 1-9 are readings of text the spec leaves open, fixed here before VAL runs.

1. "Close of the 24th 1h bar after entry": entry is the open of bar j0 (signal bar + 1h); bars j0..j0+23 are held; exit price = close of bar j0+23 (24 h after the entry open). The short-and-hold baseline uses the same close.
2. One position per coin: a signal on bar i' is taken only if i' >= the exit bar of the previous trade (a stop/target exit inside bar j is known before the close of bar j, so a signal formed at that close may open the next trade). Signals that fail the 0.5R skip never open a position.
3. DEV never loads a bar at or after 2024-01-01 (arrays are cut at the split), so DEV signals whose 24-bar exit window would cross the split (last day of 2023) are dropped, not completed with VAL prices. VAL (run later) loads all bars and takes signals with open time >= 2024-01-01; the one-position rule restarts at the split.
4. Bars: 1h/30m buckets exist only when all their 15m bars exist; a signal needs contiguous 1h bars from the signal bar through the 24th held bar, the 30m bar closing with the signal bar, and the 15m bar closing with it. Indicator series (RSI, EMA, WMA, ATR) run over the sequence of existing buckets (gaps are not filled); gaps are rare on spot.
5. Warm-up: pivots need a complete prior UTC day, HM needs 29 30m bars, ATR 15 1h bars. The secondary cache starts 2019-01-01, so signals before about 2019-01-03 are lost there; the primary sample is fetched from 2018-10-01.
6. Phase: the table label for day d is used as written. Recomputation from BTC daily closes truncated at d-1 (verify_phase, DEV days 2019-2023): every table MARKDOWN day passes the MARKDOWN precondition (434 of 434), the 44 days that pass it but are not MARKDOWN are all CAPITULATION (the spec lets it override), close_used equals the previous day's close on every row (0 mismatches). Cycle state is read from the table's `cycle` column.
7. Placebo: for each real trade the pool is bars of the same coin and same UTC hour as the real ENTRY bar, whose own UTC day is MARKDOWN, with a full contiguous 24-bar window, inside the same split; the one-position rule is not applied to placebo draws; trades with an empty pool would be dropped from both real and placebo means (count in `dropped_no_pool`, 0 on DEV). Cost in R equals 0.0012 / (stop-distance %).
8. Maker row: limit at the signal close, fills if the next bar high is strictly above it (the same bar then runs the stop/target checks, stop first); stop D, target skip and cost (6 bp) rebuilt from the limit price; fill ratio = filled / (signals passing filters and the 0.5R skip with the limit entry). Not pass-bearing.
9. Statistics: win % = share of trades with net R > 0; break-even win % = mean |net loss| / (mean net win + mean |net loss|); CI = bootstrap over signal UTC days of the ratio sum(net)/n, 5000 draws, seed 20261114; ex-best-5 = mean net R per trade after removing the 5 days with the largest summed net R. Control = same rule on days whose table label is present and not MARKDOWN.
10. CI wiring (append-only allowlists, rules followed): `test_study.py` registered in scripts/dev/extra_tests.tsv; the look-ahead lint hits in study.py (forward windows for the exit simulation, bootstrap percentile) are allowlisted with reasons; PRE_REG.md has a `## Context` block that lacks the literal `Regime:` and `Entry:` lines, so it was added to scripts/dev/prereg_context_lint_allow.tsv instead of editing the frozen spec (the lead may prefer to add those two lines before the freeze).
