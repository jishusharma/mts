## 2. Critical Gaps Found

All six code/schema/contract gaps below are synthetically reproduced without reading market data via:
`python MyTradingSys/research/trendlines_2026-10-17/repro_trendline_gaps.py`

### Gap 1 — HIGH: `price_arm="close"` is dead by construction because `_penetration_ok` and `_advance` test wicks instead of closes
- **Evidence:** `TRENDLINE_CONTRACT.md` P5 defines the close arm as *"close series with the same k and tie rule"* (from Krown's line-chart inspection, `E:62`). In `trendline_core.py:105-109`, `_px` selects `bar["c"]` for pivot anchors when `price_arm == "close"`, so the line passes through `(ia, close[ia])` and `(ib, close[ib])`. However, `_penetration_ok` (`trendline_core.py:206-217`) unconditionally tests `bar["l"] < L - tol` (support) and `bar["h"] > L + tol` (resistance) over `j in range(ia, ib + 1)` — including the anchor bars `ia` and `ib` themselves, where `L(ia) = close[ia]` and `L(ib) = close[ib]`. Any normal lower wick on a support anchor (`close[ia] - low[ia] > 0.10 * ATR_b`) immediately fails L6 penetration! Similarly, `_advance` (`trendline_core.py:248-259`) measures contacts and resets using `bar["l"]`/`bar["h"]` instead of `bar["c"]`.
- **Impact:** In `counts/quality.txt`, `close_log_age500` rejected 46,736 pairs at 1h, 10,523 at 4h, and 1,579 at 1d, leaving only **1 line at 1h, 1 at 4h, and 0 at 1d**. `PRE_REG_TRENDLINES.md:49` notes the close arm is dead under contract v1; the root cause is that `trendline_core.py` applies wick checks to a close-chart line.

### Gap 2 — HIGH: Pre-availability replay (`ib + 1 .. ib + k`) creates pre-birth touches, Arm A eligibility, and breaks
- **Evidence:** Under contract P6, pivot `b` at bar `ib` is confirmed only at the close of `t = ib + k` (`k = 3`). When `_pair` creates the line at `t = ib + k`, `trendline_core.py:199-200` replays `for j in range(ib + 1, t + 1): self._advance(ln, j)`. Thus bars `ib + 1 .. ib + k - 1` (when the line did not yet exist causally) can open a contact episode, trigger a distance-away reset (`eligible` for Arm A with `bar < known_at`), or break the line before birth.
- **Impact:** In `count_only.py:127-132`, the post-eligibility filter checks `e2["known_at"] > e["known_at"]` (`t0 = ib + k`). If a line touches on `ib + 1` and resets on `ib + 2`, it is born already `eligible` in Arm A at `known_at = ib + k` **without a single post-birth contact episode**, and any break at `ib + k + 1` is counted as an Arm A post-eligibility break. Conversely, if a real contact occurs on bar `ib + k` for an Arm V line (eligible at `known_at = ib + k`), `e2["known_at"] > t0` is `False` (`30 > 30`), dropping the contact on the confirmation bar. Contract E2/E5/B1 must specify whether state transitions (`touched`, `eligible`, `broken`) may only occur on bars `j > ib + k` (strictly after anchor confirmation), while bars `ib + 1 .. ib + k` are part of the creation viability check (like `g3_pack.py:133`).

### Gap 3 — HIGH: `_broken_anchor` (E11) globally poisons anchor `ia` across all lines on the same side
- **Evidence:** `TRENDLINE_CONTRACT.md` E11 states: *"a pivot b' formed after a line's break may not pair with that line's anchors (a broken line is not extended); pairing with other available pivots is unchanged."* In `trendline_core.py:177-178, 202-204`, `_broken_anchor(side, ia)` returns `True` if **any** broken line on that side had `ia` as either `ln["ia"]` or `ln["ib"]`.
- **Impact:** If a major swing low `ia` pairs with an early steep pullback pivot `ib1` and that steep inner line `(ia, ib1)` breaks, `ia` is permanently banned from pairing with any later higher low `ib2` to form a shallower outer trend line `(ia, ib2)`. This contradicts `REPORT.md:63` (*"Accelerating nested lines have distinct IDs; breaking a steep inner line while the outer line holds is its own context"*) and suppresses valid outer fan lines in DEV counts.

### Gap 4 — MEDIUM: `count_only.py` multi-touch inflation, uncounted `retest`/`reclaim` events, and full-sample slope terciles
- **Evidence:**
  1. `PRE_REG_TRENDLINES.md:52` frames the primary question as *"after a line is eligible (arm A or B), does the **next** contact (touch) or break change the sign-adjusted forward move..."*, and `REPORT.md:77` specifies *"first-return reaction to a legitimate line"*. However, `count_only.py:128-130` loops over **all** `e2["state"] == "touched"` events with `known_at > t0`, counting 2nd, 3rd, and later post-eligibility touch episodes on the same surviving line (inducing survival/repeated-line weighting within the cell).
  2. `trendline_core.py` emits `retested` and `reclaimed` states (contract B6/B7), and `REPORT.md:77` names break/retest as one of the three core questions, but `count_only.py` (`EVENTS = ("touch", "break")`) never counts `retested` or `reclaimed` cells, leaving their DEV sample size unknown.
  3. `count_only.py:115-119` computes `terciles(slopes)` across all lines formed over the entire 2022–2024 DEV run at the end of the pass. Using `slope_tercile` as a conditioning stratum in any outcome study requires expanding (or prior-window) tercile thresholds so a line's stratum does not depend on future lines (`repro_trendline_gaps.py` shows line `15|27` shifting `T1 -> T2` when future bars are appended).
