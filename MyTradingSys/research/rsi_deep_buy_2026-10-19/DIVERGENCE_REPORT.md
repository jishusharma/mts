## What was built

- **Detector** (SPEC Step J as patched by SHOT_REVIEW C1-C10): close pivots (strict local minimum of the close, `rsi_divergence.py:10`); a pivot at b is known at b+1, so the event is stamped at c = b+1 and filled at the open of c+1. Checked against `rsi_divergence.py` on 60m abs30, 2022-01..2023-06: all 95 P-adj events of this detector are events of the old module with the same stamp bar (the old module stamps `ts[b+1]`, not earlier than the confirming bar; OPUS_FRAMING s2.2 item 5). Episode: Stage-0 entry (RSI <= L after >= L+3) until RSI closes above 50; an entry inside a running episode belongs to it. Anchor = lowest-RSI pivot (<= L) so far, replaced by a lower one. Primary row = P-anchor, close, eps 0, min RSI diff 0, end 50. Every firing is an event, none is dropped as 'the remembered one'.
- **Control**: non-firing pivots of the same cell, RSI at the pivot within 3 points, nearest trailing ATR%, one-to-one (so the comparison is divergence vs no divergence at the same RSI level, TF and volatility). `n_matched` is smaller than `n_events` where no unused control sits inside the tolerance (1m abs10 most of all).
- **Outcome**: fill next open, exit open N bars later (N = 4 and 16 bars of the TF), fee 0.10% round trip; P(price above fill + fee), P(new lower low) (bar low below the lowest low of the episode so far), median MFE/MAE, move in ATR units normalised by the session-of-day seasonal factor (20-day causal slot mean), EV per calendar day.
- **Context vector (8, completed bars only)**: returns 1h/4h/12h/24h/7d in 1h-ATR; drawdown from the 24h high, hours since it, and the 24h sweep depth; decline speed; speed change (last 1h vs the prior 4h); 5m range z-score and slope; realised-vol percentile (30d); volume and taker-buy z; session. Walk-forward ridge logistic on 6 of them (24h return, drawdown, speed, speed change, range z, vol percentile), refit once per UTC day on events whose N=16 outcome was known, compared with the Step G level-only table ((k+1)/(n+2) on earlier resolved Stage-0 entries of the cell) and the divergence-only rate.
- **Step J extras**: pairing variants, close vs wick, eps, min RSI difference, FAILURE_SWING rows, RSI regime band (30d floor/ceiling, share under 30, dips under 40, regime break), HTF 3-week floor break, causal zigzag state on 1h/1d/1w, session clock in exchange-local time, short mirror. The five-week sanity weeks of SHOT_REVIEW (b)12 lie in HOLDOUT and were not read.
- **Step H** (confluence score) belongs to card `rsi-deep-confluence`, running in parallel; the comparison here is against level-only and divergence-only only.

## Parameters (SOURCED vs OURS)

SOURCED: RSI14 Wilder on close, UTC (econ_floor.py, FORMULAS s1); fee 0.10% round trip (backtest/fees.py); ATR = plain SMA of true range (FORMULAS s12); z-score vs the prior L bars, population sd (FORMULAS s28.1); close-to-close price basis and min RSI difference 0 (Malkan, `rsi_divergence.py:3,7`); DEV/VALIDATION/HOLDOUT dates (backtest/splits.py); >= 30 independent days, Holm, VALIDATION read once, walk-forward (SPEC guards).
OURS (every one a trial-ledger row, none tuned on VALIDATION): h = 3; episode end 50 (40, 60 trials); the four pairing rules; eps 0.05% and 0.1 ATR; min diff 2 and 5; wick basis; 24h sweep window; control match (3 RSI points, ATR%); N = 4 and 16; ridge lambda 1, +-4 sd clip, 40 prior events, daily refit; terciles; 30d RSI band; 3-week HTF floor; zigzag 3 ATR on 1h/1d/1w closes; 20-day seasonal factor; session clock (America/New_York 09:30-10:30, Europe/London 08:00-09:00).

## Caveats

- Events cluster (one slow decline produces many firings against one anchor); CIs are day-clustered, but several days of one selloff are still correlated. Cells with < 30 days are marked too_few_days and not ranked.
- `regime_break` (the pivot's RSI below the trailing 30d RSI floor) is 0 for every divergence event by construction: the 30d window contains the anchor, whose RSI is lower than the divergence pivot's. The row is void; the version that matters (anchor RSI vs the 30d floor before the episode) was not built, and `divergence_val.csv` cannot be regenerated after its one read. The HTF 3-week floor break flags (`htf_break_a/b`) are the working Step J regime-break features.
- P-anchor-split is permissive by construction (price may be lower than any swing since the anchor); its counts are an upper bound on 'the operator's two panels'.
- The matched control is a retrospective match (a control pivot may lie after the event); each control pivot is itself causal at its own stamp, so no look-ahead enters the outcome comparison.
- Step H confluence is not in the comparison (parallel card). OI is NOT_IN_DATA before 2025-09. WaveTrend agreement is NOT_IN_DATA until its inputs are pinned.
- ATR-unit columns use the 1h ATR for returns and the TF ATR times the seasonal factor for outcomes; they are scaled descriptions, not edge claims.
