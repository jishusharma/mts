# CONFIRMATIONS: Stage 3, causal confirmations at an RSI low vs the raw touch (RSI deep-buy)

BTCUSDT, DEV `confirmations_dev.csv` (open < 2024-07-01, written and frozen first, sha256 `5204f0cf36300e9b`), VALIDATION `confirmations_val.csv` read once (2024-07-01..2025-06-30, frozen pairs only). HOLDOUT (2025-07 onward) is refused by the loader and was never read. Generated by `confirmations.py`; the trial row in `layer_lab/TRIALS.md` was written before the outcomes.

Method. Unit = Stage-0 episode (RSI <= level after >= level+3) on the six TF x level cells that H1 did not kill (ECON_FLOOR.md: 60m abs20/abs30/rel, 15m abs30/abs20, 1m abs10). A confirmation fires on the first bar c within 16 bars of the signal bar, before the episode re-arms, using bars <= c only (higher-TF RSI = last completed bar before the bar's own HTF group). Entry = open of c+1, exit = open N bars later (N = 4, 16 bars of the TF), fee 0.10% round trip (SOURCED backtest/fees.py). `P(lower)` = a bar low in c+1..c+N below the lowest low of the episode so far (s..c); for the raw touch the reference is the signal bar's low, so a confirmation lowers it partly by construction (its reference low is the lower episode low). `net` = mean net return per event, % of price, with a day-clustered 95% CI. `vs raw` = net minus the unconditional raw-touch net over all episodes (independent-groups day-clustered CI, conservative since the groups overlap): the tradeable comparison. `EV/day` = sum of net returns per calendar day of the sample, 1 unit per signal. Rows with fewer than 30 independent days are `too_few_days` (kept in the CSV, not ranked).

- DEV: 228 confirmation rows (singles and the frozen pair, x cell x N), 150 with >= 30 days; net CI lower bound > 0: **0**; vs-raw CI lower bound > 0: **0**. Chance alone gives about 2.5% one-sided = 3.8 of 150.
- VALIDATION: 228 confirmation rows (singles and the frozen pair, x cell x N), 80 with >= 30 days; net CI lower bound > 0: **0**; vs-raw CI lower bound > 0: **0**. Chance alone gives about 2.5% one-sided = 2.0 of 80.

## Verdict

**No confirmation shows a fee-net edge on DEV: no row has a day-clustered net CI above 0 and none beats the unconditional raw touch with its CI above 0.**
The best DEV mean net is C8_newlow7d on 60m abs20 N=16: +0.829% [-0.77, +2.43] on 32 days; VALIDATION same row: +0.207% on 13 days (too_few_days). The ECON_FLOOR 60m abs20 lead: raw N=16 net +0.565% [-0.71, +1.84] on 41 days, too wide to call, and no confirmation narrows it enough.

What the confirmations do: they cut the odds of a new lower low (tables below) and give up part of the bounce; per event the net stays inside the fee noise. The paired `delta` columns in the CSV (confirmed entry minus raw entry on the same episodes) are positive for many rows, but they are conditional on the confirmation appearing later, so they measure what waiting saves in episodes that kept falling, not an edge; use `vs raw`.

## Notes and decisions

- Open interest: NOT_IN_DATA before 2025-09 (Binance metrics archive starts 2025-09-01, OPUS_FRAMING s3); no OI confirmation tested, row `C_OI` in both CSVs.
- Grid: 21 singles + RAW + 1 frozen pair per cell, N in 4 and 16; every row is in the CSVs, nothing dropped; the multiple-comparison count is in the bullets above.
- Not run (outside this card's list C1, C3, C5, C7, C9, C8): C4 divergence, C6 dwell, funding, candle patterns, VWAP distance.
- DECISION (OURS): confirmation window 16 bars of the TF; a bar after the episode re-arms (RSI >= level+3) cannot confirm.
- DECISION (OURS): fee charged once per trade (0.10% round trip); no slippage or funding; fill = next bar open exactly as in ECON_FLOOR.
- Overlap: events inside one day or one crash are correlated; days are the unit of evidence, the CI is day-clustered and still optimistic across adjacent days.
- Episodes whose exit falls beyond the sample end are dropped, not clipped. RSI warm-up (200 bars; the daily HTF RSI needs 200 days, so C7b on 60m is empty before mid-2022) is NaN, never a signal.
- Disclosure: the first VALIDATION run left the `vs raw` columns of the frozen PAIR rows at 0 (a bug: the raw reference was not computed for the pair call). The VALIDATION data were NOT read again; those columns were recomputed arithmetically from the RAW and PAIR rows of the same csv (mean difference, hypot of the two SEs recovered from the CIs). The same arithmetic reproduces the corrected code on DEV to 5e-6 percentage points.
- VALIDATION was read once with pairs frozen from the DEV csv; nothing was tuned on it. Guards in `test_confirmations.py`: HTF value unchanged when the current HTF bar changes, DEV loader refuses bars from 2024-07-01, entry = next open.
