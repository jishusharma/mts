# RSI deep-buy Phase 1F: context-conditioned tables (card rsi-deep-context-tables, SPEC s2 Step F)

Data: BTCUSDT 1m. DEV = bar open < 2024-07-01 (`context_tables_dev.csv`, written and frozen first, sha256 `a8e3ce293dcd01a4`). VALIDATION = events 2024-07-01..2025-06-30, read once (`context_tables_val.csv`); its parameters (m, candidate list) come from the frozen DEV file. HOLDOUT (2025-07 onward) is refused by the loader and was never read. Generated by `context_tables.py`; test: `test_context_tables.py`. Descriptive probabilities, not a trading rule; no fee-net edge is claimed.

## What was built

Event = RSI(tf) closes at or below level L (10..40 step 5) after being 3+ above; fill = next bar open. Two outcomes: `price` = open 60 minutes after the fill exceeds the fill by more than the 0.10% round-trip fee; `rsi` = RSI rises 5 points off its running minimum before that minimum is 3 below the entry RSI, within 60 bars (unresolved events excluded). F0 (unconditional baseline, the table of `PROB_REPORT.md` made walk-forward) = (k+1)/(n+2) on the earlier events of the same (tf, level) whose outcome was already resolved. A context cell (scheme, state, tf, level) is shrunk to that F0 value, p = (k + m p0)/(n + m), and returns the F0 value while it has fewer than 30 distinct past days. Every prediction uses only events resolved at or before its signal bar (walk-forward, so VALIDATION is a forward test of the whole pipeline).

Context schemes (21): macro `M_trend` (30d return sign and close vs 200d mean: down / mixed / up), `M_vol` (tercile of 30d realised vol against its own expanding history), `M_range` (tercile of the 30d range width against its own history; low = consolidation); all from the last COMPLETED daily bar, NA before 2023-01-01 (2022 is warm-up). Trailing context per lookback 4h, 6h, 12h, 1d, 3d, 1w: `floor_L` (event RSI minus the lowest RSI of the previous L: below the floor = new regime / within 3 points / more than 3 above), `touch_L` (earlier events of that level inside L: 0 / 1 / 2+), `bounce_L` (of those, the share whose price bounce beat the fee and was already resolved: none / under 50% / 50% or more).

## Result

**4 of 168 pre-registered context tests are kept** (DEV: delta log-loss > 0 and Holm p < 0.05 over all 168; then VALIDATION delta log-loss > 0). DEV candidates: 5. Everything else: F0 is the answer.

Read this honestly: `keep` only means the VALIDATION delta is above 0, as the card's rule asks. The kept contexts' VALIDATION t over days is 0.60, 0.08, 0.60, 1.26 (none reaches 2) and the gains are a few 1e-4 nats of log-loss per event, so no context is a material improvement over F0; F0 stays the working table and a kept context is a candidate for the next card's fee-net EV check, not a rule.

delta = mean log-loss of F0 minus mean log-loss of the context prediction per scored event (positive = context better). t = over distinct days (events of a day cluster); p one-sided normal; p_holm over the full grid. share_ctx = scored events that had >= 30 context days (the rest fell back to the parent).

## Parameters (SOURCED vs OURS)

SOURCED: RSI14 Wilder on close, UTC (econ_floor.py, FORMULAS.md s1); fee 0.10% round trip (backtest/fees.py); DEV/VALIDATION/HOLDOUT dates (SPEC s5); >= 30 independent days per cell, empirical-Bayes shrinkage to the parent, Holm over the grid, walk-forward estimation, VALIDATION read once (SPEC Step F guards).

OURS (all in the trial ledger layer_lab/TRIALS.md, none tuned on VALIDATION): hysteresis 3; levels 10..40 step 5; price horizon 60 minutes; RSI race b=5, d=3, N=60 bars; macro definitions and cut points (terciles of own history, 200d mean, 30d windows); lookbacks 4h..1w; floor-gap cut points 0 and 3 RSI points; touch and bounce-record buckets; Laplace parent (k+1)/(n+2); m by method of moments (clamp 2..5000, estimated on DEV only); TFs 1m/5m/15m/1h.

## Caveats

- Look-ahead: every feature uses data strictly before the event bar (trailing floor shifted one bar, macro labels from the last completed day, bounce record only from outcomes resolved at or before the signal bar); tests plant a future bar and require unchanged output.
- The bounce-record feature uses the price outcome for both outcome tables.
- NOT built here: the memory-length comparison of SPEC Step F (expanding vs 1y/90d/30d P_touch calibration) and the fee-net EV comparison; this card compares log-loss only.
- Macro labels need about 330 days of history for the range scheme; 2022 is warm-up, so all scored events are 2023-01 or later.
- Holm counts the 168 summary tests; per-cell rows are not tests. About 5% of unadjusted tests are positive by chance.
