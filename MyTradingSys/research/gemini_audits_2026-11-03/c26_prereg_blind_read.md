- Multiple-testing adjustment over a Holm/Bonferroni family of size $m$ (here $m = 4$, OURS):
  - Per-hypothesis two-sided tail probability is $\alpha / (2m) = 0.05 / (2 \times 4) = 0.00625$ (OURS).

- Minimum Detectable Effect ($\text{MDE}$, net R) at sample size $n$ and per-event R standard deviation $\text{SD}$:
  $$\text{MDE}(n) = \frac{(z_{\text{holm}}(m) + z_{1-\beta}) \cdot \text{SD}}{\sqrt{n}}$$
- Total required gross edge ($\text{total}$, R) and power-gate pass condition against maximum plausible gross edge $G_{\max} = 0.30\text{ R}$ (OURS, `scripts/dev/prereg_gate.py:21`):
  $$\text{total}(n) = \text{MDE}(n) + \text{cost}, \qquad \text{PASS iff } \text{total}(n) \le 0.30\text{ R (else BLOCK).}$$
- Required sample size $n_{\text{needed}}$ so that $\text{MDE}(n) + \text{cost} \le 0.30\text{ R}$, i.e., net detectable effect $\delta = 0.30 - \text{cost}$:
  $$n_{\text{needed}} = \left(\frac{(z_{\text{holm}}(m) + z_{1-\beta}) \cdot \text{SD}}{0.30 - \text{cost}}\right)^2$$
- Supporting definitions from `FORMULAS.md`:
  - Population standard deviation divides by $N$, not $N-1$ (OURS, `FORMULAS.md:181-182`, quote: ``mean(x) / population_stdev(x) (divides by `n`, not `n-1` — population, not sample, standard deviation``; also `FORMULAS.md:556`).
