## 3. Backtest Walkthrough & Execution Mechanics

### 3.1 Setup Framework (Multi-Timeframe Smart Money Concepts / Liquidity Model)
1. **Higher Timeframe Bias (Daily / Weekly):**
   - The trader establishes macro direction using Daily structure. If Daily is bullish, trade filter enforces long positions exclusively.
   - Overhead liquidity pools (prior daily swing highs) serve as the macroscopic draw on liquidity (take-profit targets).
2. **Intermediate Framing (1 Hour):**
   - External swing structure vs internal structure points are mapped.
   - Low liquidity grabs: price sweeps a prior swing low into a higher-timeframe demand block.
   - Bullish market structure shift (BOS / CHoCH) on the 1H confirms buyer presence.
3. **Execution Timeframe (15 Minute):**
   - Wait for a reaction inside the 1H point of interest (POI).
   - Require an internal liquidity sweep on 15m (taking out recent lows).
   - Require a 15m bullish break of structure leaving behind a fresh, unmitigated 15m demand block.
   - Place a limit order on the demand block retest.
4. **Risk & Trade Management:**
   - Stop loss placed strictly below the 15m demand base / structural swing low.
   - Minimum target is 1:3 Risk-to-Reward ratio (or the opposing HTF liquidity pool).

### 3.2 Individual Trade Logs (January 2024 on FX Replay)
- **Trade 1 (Long):** Entry after 1H liquidity sweep into daily demand, 15m bullish flip, retest of 15m demand. Target 1:3 hit cleanly. **Result: Win (+3.0R / ~+3.8% account gain).**
- **Trade 2 (Long):** Setup identified after 15m break of structure following a demand sweep. Stop loss was set aggressively tight below the demand wick to achieve 1:3 RR. Price dipped and stopped out the position by exactly 1 pip before aggressively expanding straight into the profit target. **Result: Loss (-1.0R / -1.0% account loss).**
- **Trade 3 (Long):** Clean entry upon retest of newly formed demand zone following continuation break of structure. Hit take-profit target at overhead swing high. **Result: Win (+3.0R / ~+2.9% account gain).**
