## Definitions (from the quotes above)

- **1/2/3-bar indicator (SOURCED):** a mechanical trend indicator (Gann Swing Pro) that shows the trend state, up, down or neutral, for three nested time frames. 1-bar is the low-term, 2-bar the mid-term, 3-bar the high-term. Written as '1-day 1-bar'. The label '1-day' appears in front, so the bar sizes are likely 1-bar = the daily swing, 2-bar and 3-bar = higher swing degrees; exact bar sizes are NOT_IN_DATA.
- **Confluence (SOURCED):** all three bars agree (all up or all down) = strong bias; mixed = indecision.
- **Lead-lag (SOURCED):** the 1-bar (and 2-bar) turn first; their turn is 'always a precursor' that the next higher time frame turns.
- **Trigger level (SOURCED):** he gives the price where the 1-bar trend flips (e.g. above 61,200 flips the 1-bar up), then the next pivot point as the target (63,000). Higher-time-frame confirmation needs closes above the pivot (weekly).
- **50% level (SOURCED):** midpoint of the major prior run; as support it is held, a change of trend beneath it is the lead for weakness; as resistance a retest from below tends to fail.
- **Pivot point (SOURCED, loose):** a named price level (swing high/low or breakout zone) such as 83,000, 84,300, 88, 63,000; breakdown beneath it is the early lead; confluence when it coincides with the 50% level.
- **Retest (SOURCED, loose):** price returns to a broken level or to the 50% level; hold (closes beyond) = continuation, rejection = reversal.
