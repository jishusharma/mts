## Definitions (from the quotes above, in plain words)

- **Three-bar signal (SOURCED):** three consecutive bars in one direction (higher highs and higher lows after a low; lower highs and lower lows after a high) counted from a significant turning point. Works on daily, 3-day, weekly. Short-term signal; he reads it as a probability shift, not a trade rule.
- **Significance of the turning point (SOURCED):** 'generally means all-time high, but not always' (GaHjNt91mPU).
- **50% correction (SOURCED):** the halfway point of the prior move (top and bottom averaged). Above = strong half, below = weak half. Hold above 50% after a pullback = strength; rejection off 50% = correction continues.
- **Retest (SOURCED, loose):** price comes back to a broken level or to the 50% and the reaction is read: retest of the 50 from underneath after a breakdown = weak.
- **Confluence (SOURCED):** he stacks 50% with moving averages (100-week, 200-day, 50-week) and swing tops, e.g. BTC 92k = 50% + 100-week near 90k + 98k monthly lower swing top (4kpH94Hb0GQ).
