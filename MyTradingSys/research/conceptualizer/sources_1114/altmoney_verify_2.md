## Missed
1. Sell-side friction: honeypot/sell-tax tokens, slippage and thin liquidity mean the paper gain is not the cash-out gain; position size vs pool depth.
2. Wallet drainers / fake links / approvals: small buyers lose to scams (>= $14B) more than to price.
3. India-specific: 30% tax on gains with no offset plus 1% TDS makes high-turnover memecoin trading structurally negative; losers cannot recover tax.
4. Survivorship: CoinGecko survival stats and 1000x stories ignore the 95%+ that died; base rate dominates.
