## 3. FAIL rule (fixed now, all OURS, read on Deribit testnet fills only)
Per trade: risk USD = |fill - stop| / fill x size USD at entry (the plan's invalidation, d-stops-follow-trade-1106); net R = realised PnL in USD after the venue's actual fees and funding / risk USD. A stop fill is about -1 R, a target fill about +2 R minus fees. Readout weekly (card krown-s8-forward-weekly-readout), cumulative since start, BTC only until ETH joins.
- **FAIL-A drawdown:** cumulative fee-net R falls 12 R below its running peak (= 12 straight stops at a 2 R target; equals the FAIL-B depth so neither rule is looser).
- **FAIL-B streak:** 12 consecutive fee-net losers (P = 0.6 % at a 35 % win rate, 0.2 % at 40 %).
- **FAIL-C expectancy:** n >= 30 filled trades AND mean net R + 1.28 x SE < 0 (the one-sided 90 % upper bound is below zero; SE = sd / sqrt(n) over trades, sd with ddof = 1, plain, no bootstrap at this n).
- **PARITY-FAIL (a counted fail of the build, not of the edge):** any live entry whose `(t_close, side, stop)` is not in the replay's event set, or any replay event (bar closed inside the readout's UTC week, Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00) with no alert row and no `skipped_busy` / `skipped_floor` row. The coordinator pauses the source (blocklist entry, DEPLOY_RUNBOOK) until the parity card fixes it. The trades stay in A-C at their realised net R (a non-replay loser counts; nothing leaves the kill count), and the readout marks them `non_replay`.
Any FAIL: the readout writes `FAIL <A|B|C>` in `FORWARD_LOG.csv`, files the stop (blocklist + reason) as a card for the coordinator, records when it failed and the phase tag (that is the record d-phase-first-method-1106 asks for), and the family keeps the R6 PRE_REG 0f kill scope: no redesign of S8 on 4h BTC/ETH/SOL with this stop. No PASS exists in this test and no positive label either: the readout prints n, mean net R, the one-sided 90 % lower bound (mean - 1.28 SE) and the FAIL-C upper bound as numbers, nothing else. Testnet fill quality vs the taker + 0.02 % slip model is a check column (mean slip bp per leg), never an input to A-C. Mainnet needs the fee-net OOS record and the operator (Hard rules); nothing here promotes.
