## 2. What it says in one line
No table predicts direction beyond cost (row 2 is the only fee-net test and it is KILL; rows 3-7 are descriptive by design). Their value is context for a trade already chosen by phase and setup: how often this state continues, how far a move runs past a level before it comes back, how often a level holds, how likely the open candle keeps its colour. That is what a leveraged directional trade needs for its stop, target and size. It was never going to be a signal (BRIEF.md s6; d-phase-first-method-1106).
