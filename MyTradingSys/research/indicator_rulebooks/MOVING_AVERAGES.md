**Result: Moving averages smooth price series to classify directional trend and dynamic support/resistance zones. The creator canon treats moving averages strictly as filters and context rather than standalone triggers; our empirical tests confirm classic crossovers and raw MA levels bleed fees across intraday frames (0 of 384 intraday MA rules profitable), daily SMA200 long-flat Alpha fails validation, and only short-horizon (~1-week) price-to-MA distance retains peer-reviewed out-of-sample predictability.**

## 1. What it measures
Measures the smoothed central tendency of closing prices over trailing rolling windows, filtering high-frequency market noise to reveal underlying trend direction, momentum persistence, and dynamic support/resistance boundaries.
Formula pointer: `backtest/layers/FORMULAS.md` section 3 (SMA: rolling arithmetic mean, `VERIFIED-TV 2026-10-17`), section 4 (EMA: exponential recursive filter with smoothing multiplier $\alpha = 2/(N+1)$ seeded from the initial N-bar SMA, `VERIFIED-TV 2026-10-17`), and section 21.6 (Krown moving averages and 5 EMA leash, `VERIFIED-TV 2026-10-17`). Code modules: `research/ma_layer_2026-11/ma_state.py`, `research/ma_layer_2026-11/cycle_ma_clock.py`, and `tools/live_aid/ma_strip.js`.

## 7. Confluence role: TREND GATE
Moving averages function strictly as a **TREND GATE** and higher-timeframe regime context filter.
- What it may add confidence to:
  - May add confidence to a long breakout, pullback, or value-area reclaim setup when price is stacked above rising 21/55 EMAs and the 200 SMA, confirming macro flow alignment.
  - May serve as an entry veto: strictly vetoing aggressive long entries when price is trapped below a downward-sloping 200 SMA or following a confirmed death cross (Krown rule KR-M07; CCU bear transition).
  - May provide dynamic location context when a confirmed order-flow absorption signal (CVD absorption or footprint delta reversal) occurs at the first touch of the 200 SMA or within the 21/55 EMA zone after a long absence (KR-M03, KR-M05).
- Rule in one line: Never trade raw moving average crosses or bare touches as standalone entries; require higher-timeframe MA slope alignment as a directional trend gate while requiring independent volume/orderflow confirmation at the zone. Today it is CONTEXT ONLY.
