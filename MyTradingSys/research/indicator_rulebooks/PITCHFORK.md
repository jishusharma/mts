## 1. What it measures
A dynamic trend channel and equilibrium axis constructed from three alternating swing pivots (P0 anchor origin, P1 reaction extreme 1, P2 retracement extreme 2). The Median Line (ML) is the ray drawn from P0 passing through the midpoint M of P1 and P2; the Upper Median Line (UML) and Lower Median Line (LML) run parallel to the ML through P1 and P2 respectively.
- Formula pointer: `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/FORMULAS.md` Section 19 specifies 2-anchor trend-lines, while median-line 3-pivot pitchfork geometry is implemented directly in code: `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/pitchfork/pitchfork_detector.py:66-149` (audited with zero difference in `research/gemini_audits_2026-10-30/review_gpt_formula_audits.md:28`).

## 2. Developer's own rules (SOURCED)
- **Developer:** Dr. Alan Hall Andrews (1900–1985), MIT-educated engineer and mathematician, adapting Roger W. Babson's Newtonian action-reaction principles into the *Babson Action-Reaction Course* (1960–1975) [SOURCED: `papers/01_MASTERS_PITCHFORK_FOUNDATIONS.md:26-28`].
- **Five Core Rules of Dr. Alan Andrews:**
  1. *Rule 1 (The 80% Rule / Magnet Effect):* Price will reach the Median Line approximately 80% of the time following the completion of Pivot 2 [SOURCED: `papers/01_MASTERS_PITCHFORK_FOUNDATIONS.md:83`, Alan H. Andrews via `MyTradingSys/research/pitchfork_2026-10-19/RESEARCH_PITCHFORK_LAYER.md:34`].
  2. *Rule 2 (Reversal or Acceleration):* When price reaches the Median Line, it will either form a swing pivot reversal back toward the outer boundary (UML or LML) or accelerate through the Median Line with expanding volume/momentum toward the opposite tine [SOURCED: `papers/01_MASTERS_PITCHFORK_FOUNDATIONS.md:88-91`].
  3. *Rule 3 (Multiple Fluctuations):* Price frequently oscillates back and forth around the Median Line multiple times before concluding the trend cycle [SOURCED: `papers/01_MASTERS_PITCHFORK_FOUNDATIONS.md:93-94`].
  4. *Rule 4 (Hagopian's Rule / Failure Setup):* If price reverses before reaching the Median Line, it signals an acute imbalance of order flow; price will accelerate violently in the opposite direction and break completely out through the opposite parallel tine [SOURCED: `papers/01_MASTERS_PITCHFORK_FOUNDATIONS.md:97-99`, Alan H. Andrews via `MyTradingSys/research/pitchfork_2026-10-19/RESEARCH_PITCHFORK_LAYER.md:39`].
  5. *Rule 5 (Action-Reaction Lines):* Any pivot line can serve as a Center Line (CL); prior swing extrema on the action side project equidistant parallel lines on the reaction side where future turning points cluster [SOURCED: `papers/01_MASTERS_PITCHFORK_FOUNDATIONS.md:101-103`].
- **Geometric Variations:**
  - *Jerome Schiff (Schiff Pitchfork):* Designed to solve excessive median line slope (>55°-60°) following shallow retracements. Shifts anchor P0 50% in time and 50% in price toward P1: $t_{0,\text{Schiff}} = \frac{t_0 + t_1}{2}, p_{0,\text{Schiff}} = \frac{p_0 + p_1}{2}$ [SOURCED: `papers/01_MASTERS_PITCHFORK_FOUNDATIONS.md:121-128`, Jerome Schiff].
  - *Modified Schiff Pitchfork:* Popularized in futures charting; maintains origin time coordinate $t_0$, but shifts price to the midpoint between P0 and P1: $t_{0,\text{ModSchiff}} = t_0, p_{0,\text{ModSchiff}} = \frac{p_0 + p_1}{2}$ [SOURCED: `papers/01_MASTERS_PITCHFORK_FOUNDATIONS.md:131-134`]. (Note: TradingView UI labels shift in both time and price as Modified Schiff, whereas classic literature labels time-shift as standard Schiff and vertical-only shift as Modified Schiff).
- **Stops and Targets:** Stop-loss placed 1.0 ATR beyond Pivot 2; profit target 1 at the Median Line (ML), profit target 2 at the opposite tine [SOURCED: `papers/01_MASTERS_PITCHFORK_FOUNDATIONS.md:185-186`]. Hagopian failure target is a measured move equal to channel width $W = |p_{UML} - p_{ML}|$ [SOURCED: `MyTradingSys/research/pitchfork_2026-10-19/RESEARCH_PITCHFORK_LAYER.md:42`].

## 7. Confluence role
**LEVEL**
- What it adds confidence to: Provides dynamic support/resistance corridor boundaries and dynamic fair-value reference (Median Line). Outer tines (LML for long, UML for short) define dynamic location for resting limit order placement or mean-reversion fades; Median Line defines the primary reversion target; Hagopian failures provide directional momentum continuation signals.
- Rule: Pitchfork is never a standalone entry trigger. It functions as a **Level / Location Filter** requiring microstructural confirmation (e.g. Volume Profile Value Area confluence, passive delta absorption, or clean reversal candle close) before entering.
