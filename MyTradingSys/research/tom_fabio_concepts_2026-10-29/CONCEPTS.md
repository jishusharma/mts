## A. TOM in plain steps

**Core idea.** Markets are auctions; big players leave their footprints as volume at price; those prices make reactions. Tom plans a route
from one volume area to the next and only acts when the market proves the idea. He never claims a score.
"First, you have an idea. Second, you have proof that it's really time now to implement the idea. And third, you have a really clean risk management so that the math is on your side." [TOM-068 zQTbdEweG3c:19]

### A1. How he reads context (always first)
1. Trend or balance? "Whether it's trend or balance because I have to use different setups there and that is absolutely crucial." [TOM-065 Ld1dhjGdTCA:19]
   He says balance is the common state: "markets are only about 30% truly stably trending. And about 70% they're just not." [TOM-046 43JaKHRvxHk:19] (his own statistic, unverified).
2. Higher-timeframe profiles for direction of money flow: "try drawing a trend based only on the V POX and the value areas" [TOM-053 Ld1dhjGdTCA:19] (ASR spelling of VPOC).
3. Where the market opened relative to yesterday's value, and how big the opening range is: "Open range, meaning the opening range. If it's very large, it likely won't hold. If it's very small, it likely will." [TOM-207 Fj4FDZQxw0I:19]
4. Liquidity (volume) state. "And during lunchtime, there's usually relatively little volume in the market." [TOM-029 wPMiyJXL0zU:19]; liquidity leaving/returning is read as proof (A3).
5. After a big move he waits: "when such a balance profile comes after a strong trend, then it means the market is starting to sort itself out. That doesn't mean it's going short yet." [TOM-058 zQTbdEweG3c:19]

### A2. The map (zones he draws)
- POC / value area high-low / low-volume areas / overlaps: "where high volume occurs, we often see a reaction when a market returns there." [TOM-182 NkQeOVDTAec:19]
- Empty areas get filled: "Then you see here is the low volume area." [TOM-211 4MoNjEQ2Dd8:19] (then the market is "filling this empty price area with liquidity").
- Gaps and fair value gaps are conditional: "fair value gaps are great support resistance levels. They are, but only if the market has trend stability." [TOM-204 Fj4FDZQxw0I:19]
- Below a value edge "Very often it accelerates at first. And why is that the case? because a lot of market participants fall out of balance" [TOM-186 YmygDgtoxO8:19]

### A4. The proof (what makes the idea "go")
- Idea alone is not a trade: "I need an idea for a route plan and I need proof that this idea should be put into action right now." [TOM-135 NkQeOVDTAec:19]
- What counts as proof (his words): rejection and its speed - "the speed with which it rejected this is promising for me" [TOM-010 k0zm5NYn0IQ:19]; absorption -
  "It means that there is a very large buyer here who is absorbing everything that's being sold." [TOM-025 y67l86cSqb8:19]; finished vs unfinished auction (footprint) -
  "When there are high volumes at the tops or lows, the auctions are very often not yet finished, not yet completed." [TOM-013 oa8kxSLLkAo:19] and
  "there was only one side of the price with a number greater than zero. And in such phases, you can simply say there was no more interest here." [TOM-009 oa8kxSLLkAo:19];
  speed at the break - "The speed is picking up. Look at what's being bought here. And now comes the breakout." [TOM-027 4MoNjEQ2Dd8:19]
- A setup is anything that shrinks risk: "A setup is nothing more than a formation of price or volume or a footprint or whatever that gives me an advantage in keeping my risk smaller." [TOM-127 NkQeOVDTAec:19]
- He is allowed to wait for the proof: "Sure, the very brave ones just buy here, but you can also wait until the proof arrives." [TOM-161 qLnQan0KGmQ:19]

## B. FABIO in plain steps

**Core idea.** Price shows what happened; order flow shows who is pushing and whether it works. He asks: who controls the auction right now,
and is the effort rewarded? "my type of analysis is not subjective what i do can be done from any one of you okay because it's visible" [Is1HvdfQm5c:130-131] (ASR).

2. Profile framing = area qualification and bias. Value area and POC: "High execution mean strong price acceptance." [Pz8f0wWW12M:473-475]; leaving value: "if we manage to go out from the value area, usually it's where trend start." [Pz8f0wWW12M:448-450]. ### B3. Picking the side
1. Who controls the auction: "who is controlling the auction" [Is1HvdfQm5c:777]. Buyers' control = long bias; do not trade against the bias [Is1HvdfQm5c:737].
2. Pressure leads: flat price with CVD running one way = accumulation/distribution; "we know that this is a sell model day and we continue to sell" [0jM5Y31YJak:4].
3. Absorption sets direction: "we have a lower low on the cumulative volume delta here while the market is still on the same level as before. This is what it's called it's called absorption." [o-w5Gxss6T0:111-114]. Passive buyers absorbing aggressive sellers = long side.
4. At an extreme in balance: fade absorbed aggression (sniper) or wait for the other side to take control (confirmed fade) [B5]; after a breakout: join the side whose aggression is rewarded.

### B4. Triggers: aggressive flow, effort vs result
- Effort, no result = absorption: "the result of the price for a pressure in by delta was almost zero. So this is a confirmation that the buyers are punching a wall and I want to be short in this position." [0jM5Y31YJak:4]
- Effort with result = control: "you can see the punch to the wall I marked for you and you can see the rewarded effort of the sellers." [8CWKfoSJu3c:41-44]
- The pattern he says made him the most money: "you have an absorption at the top okay you have an aggression that is rewarded so these buyers no follow through the aggression rewarded and the buyers again absorbed you have three order flow information going in the same direction" [Is1HvdfQm5c:140-142]
- Entry on first follow-up: "the first moment that we had some kind of follow up on volume and confirmation from price was exactly this candle" [0jM5Y31YJak:4]
- Big traders are filtered from small: "you can filter the small traders that doesn't mean much with the big traders" [o-w5Gxss6T0:9-10]
- Failed auction here means a break attempt that gets absorbed, then the other side's aggression: "capitalize on the failed auction of the seller and then aggression of the level of the buyers" [fZlNGWvd2Ko:39-42]

## G. Findings: where the extracted rules / engine misread the concept
1. Neither trader gives an intensity scale. Tom's strength words are "idea", "proof", "many parameters" [TOM-068, TOM-139]; Fabio's is "strong buy area" [Is1HvdfQm5c:601-602]. The 1-5 ladders above are OURS; the engine's 50+7n+10 (`SetupCandidatesBuilder.java:188-197`) is also OURS and omits regime, room-to-target and counter-trend, which Tom treats as the main conviction inputs.
2. Tom's "finished auction" is a footprint fact (a bar edge where only one side printed, [TOM-009]); the engine's finished_auction is a 1m delta spike followed by reversal (`SetupCandidatesBuilder.java:791-808`). Same name, different thing; true per-price data now exists in `orderflow_live`.
3. Tom chooses the setup family by regime first [TOM-065, TOM-066]; the engine chooses direction by location (`:306-327`) and builds no breakout and no short trend-continuation (`:464-499`); in a healthy trend a VAH touch returns no setup instead of a continuation.
4. LVNs are computed live but never used (`MarketDataSnapshot.java:678-683`), Tom's B/P shape is absent (`:645-651`), and acceptance (his main filter against spikes [TOM-019]) is not implemented. In addition the snapshot lists only the 3 highest HVN prices and the 3 lowest LVN prices (`:681,683`: sorted then limit 3), not the nearest or strongest (appears to be a defect; verify before use).
5. FABIO_L1's "volume FVG = random control" verdict (FABIO_L1_REPORT.md:62) used volume spread uniformly over each bar (`fabio_volume_fvg.py:11-15`), which cannot produce Fabio's per-price lowest-volume level; FAB-006 (price rejects at that level: "this is exactly where the price will reject." [o-w5Gxss6T0:83-84]) is therefore not tested by it. FABIO_OF tested LVN triggers with real footprint but with fixed 3R/BE management, while Fabio's edge is management (FABIO_TRADE_MODEL.md section 3). A true-footprint test of the lowest-volume-bin idea is open and now possible (OURS proposal, not run here).
6. Fabio's LVN is a pivot inside a trend ("rebalance the low volume node before continuing" [Pz8f0wWW12M:518-520]), not a fade level; a balance-fade trigger at LVN misses that use.
7. Tom's minimum R must come from the trader's own hit rate [TOM-093]; the engine uses a fixed floor file. Adds are not executed for either trader (D1, D2).
8. Both concepts can run read-only beside live mode from files + public feeds (D3); the only hard gap is a live per-price footprint view, available from the CSV writer at 1-minute granularity.
