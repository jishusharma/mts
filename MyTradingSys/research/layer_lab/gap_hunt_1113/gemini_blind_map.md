## Overview of Methodological Framework

The Tom Vorwald and Fabio Valentini trading methodology centers on auction market principles, volume profiling, and asymmetrical risk management:
1. **Market Profiling & Value Areas:** Markets trend only ~30% of the time, while ~70% is spent in balance/consolidation. Trading requires identifying fair price vs. unfair prices (excess/single prints).
2. **Volume Profile & Liquidity:** >95% of observable volume consists of passive limit orders. Key inflection zones include Point of Control (POC), High Volume Nodes (magnets/targets), and Low Volume Areas / Single Prints (rejection zones).
3. **Two-Tool Engine:** The World Championship edge utilizes Value Area / Volume Profile structures combined with Footprint / Orderflow execution for confirmation.
4. **Decision Steps:**
   - **Entry:** Pullback or counter-trend entries taken only upon 'proof' (absorption / balance shift) at high volume or unfair price zones, never blind guessing.
   - **Stop Loss:** Anchored strictly behind structural invalidation levels (e.g. beyond single print or previous day value area extremes). Never traded without a stop loss.
   - **Target:** Defined mechanically at prior POCs, opposite Value Area boundaries, or high volume nodes; taking profits a few points before crowded levels.
   - **Timeframe:** Macro structural context set on 30-minute / daily charts; execution refined on 5-minute charts (never trading 5-minute in isolation).
   - **Filters:** VIX regime (>20 denotes high risk/capital flight), economic news timing (e.g., 14:30 releases), index concentration (Mag 7 >30% weight in S&P/Nasdaq), and liquid session hours (08:30–17:35).
   - **Management:** Strict mathematical expectancy with asymmetric risk-reward ratios (typically 2.5:1 up to 6:1 or higher), enabling long-term profitability even at 30%–40% hit rates across samples of 200–300 trades.
   - **No-Trade:** Trading without macro context (flying blind on 5-minute charts), fading strong trend days (~20% of sessions) with naive counter-trend tools, or holding through unhedged leveraged overnight gap risk.
