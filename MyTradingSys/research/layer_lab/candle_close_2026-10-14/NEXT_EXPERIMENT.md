# Proposed correction to the next experiment: frozen before another price run

2026-10-14. Direction of the experiment is frozen here; this document is not a complete runnable pre-registration. No new performance run is justified until the phase definitions and exact matching specification pass readiness review. All choices are OURS.

1. Start with a chronological ledger of first eligible level encounters. Each encounter has its level's availability timestamp, level family/high-or-low identity, approach direction and observable phase state. Include encounters that never develop a pattern and patterns that later fail. Freeze episode termination/re-entry rules before building the ledger; do not select episodes from later successful reversals.
2. Give each nested confirmation and its control the same decision timestamp. Compare a reclaim versus no additional signature at that timestamp, then structure/higher-low confirmations with controls subject to the same elapsed opportunity. Counting only completed confirmations hides missed trades and creates unequal opportunity windows. Confirmation-delay and missed-encounter rates are mandatory outputs.
3. Phase is multi-axis: directional structure, trend efficiency, actual bounded-range evidence, volatility and higher/lower-timeframe alignment. UNKNOWN/transition is valid. Existing 30-day labels may be retained as baseline features with accurate names; no automatic label implies permission to trade. Choose exact causal definitions on development history only and log every alternative.
4. Match controls on past-only level distance, side/approach, volatility, session and phase. Compare first eligible encounters under the same sampling rule; also test random levels at matched distances to isolate level skill. Randomisation must respect episodes and common calendar blocks. Never match on future path or future outcome.
5. Predeclare a small joint-context hypothesis set before inspecting outcome tables. No sweeping every phase combination and presenting the best as discovery. Report all cells, independent episode/day counts, uncertainty and the full attempted family size. Existing phase-positive cells are discoveries from inspected DEV and require new evidence.
6. Verify level/phase availability, control means, episode completeness, and inference independently. Delete/perturb future inputs and require identical historical features. Historical outcomes may change; historical decisions may not. Add a real application-import failure test and run the harness where its numerical dependencies are installed.
7. Separate one local-reaction endpoint from one swing-persistence endpoint; exact horizons/barriers must be frozen in the runnable pre-registration. Apply censoring, label-boundary purging, adverse/favourable excursions and execution-cost assessment. Do not convert a one-hour positive return into a bottom probability.
8. Advance to out-of-sample evaluation only if the entire phase-classifier-plus-signal policy passes DEV evidence gates. Do not spend an existing sealed window while fixing the classifier, controls or costs. Cycle-scale claims remain a separate sample-limited study.

Done when the follow-up design supplies exact equations/thresholds, encounter timing, controls, data lineage, trial family, power, costs and all 12 readiness answers. Until then the next action is design/verification repair, not another performance run.
