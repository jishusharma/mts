## What I correct in my own previous answer

1. **The proposed location-conditioned flow experiment is a justified candidate, not a proven missing ingredient for profit.** TOM_ON_BTC.md:97-98 and orderflow_views RESULT.md:56 leave that question open. Neither establishes a positive effect. Simpler price/context rules, slower horizons or another hypothesis may be better. The necessity is a falsifiable costed experiment; this particular experiment is optional.
2. **Do not conflate a stop filter with widening stops.** tasks/tom-v2-btc-min-stop-width.json explicitly drops narrow-stop signals and forbids widening. Its note already removes the DAX wait, whereas depends_on/goal retain it. This is an internally inconsistent card, not evidence its author wanted to revive DAX. The earlier general warning was not a finding about that implementation. A fee/R cap also cannot prove profitability: round-trip fee fraction c divided by stop fraction w is c/w; making w larger lowers fee/R while changing payoff and hit probability. Net expectancy must still be measured. The 0.25 R cap is a design choice, not a market law.
3. **Overlapping control samples do not automatically exaggerate significance.** The omission of covariance can increase or decrease uncertainty; positive covariance between the two means reduces variance of their difference. Independent cooldowns complicate the comparison further. Withdraw any implication that correcting it necessarily uncovers an edge or increases the standard error. Paired testing is appropriate only when genuinely paired observations/policy outcomes are defined; it is not a universal replacement.
4. **Missing human labels need not stop all implementation.** T4 asks for mechanical concept flags as well as referencing eye labels. Frozen operational definitions plus sufficient inputs could replace manual labels, as the existing blocked report itself allows. P10 remains a separate data requirement. A schema/fixture scaffold can be built without market outcomes. The exact correction is explicit input contracts and prerequisites, not a blanket demand for manual labeling of every chart.
5. **Exposure is not proof of mechanical leakage.** Reusing dates across unrelated frozen studies is not automatically invalid. The risk is adaptive shared research choices after seeing outcomes; an access/decision log establishes whether confirmation is genuinely independent. No claim that every historical result is unusable. Project-wide trial counts alone also do not calibrate a correction: correlations and the actual selection process matter.
6. **Narrow execution claims to the code inspected.** The main Tom simulator already handles gaps through stops and supports explicit slippage. Zero default slippage is an optimistic assumption, not proof every caller uses zero. Its touch-only entry still needs sensitivity checks before an execution claim. The separate divergence study has a specific gap defect demonstrated below.
7. **Independent-model review is not independent ground truth.** Two models can share a mistaken spec, translated quote or selected example. Source fidelity, independent arithmetic and unseen data matter more than which model reviews. Historical 0/7 recall, sparse 0/62 screens, and 13 passing candle tests retain their previously stated limited interpretations. I have not independently reconstructed those historical outcomes here.

## Fundamental issues I underweighted

**Economic target and benchmark.** Positive per-trade R is not sufficient to choose a strategy: define the intended venue, capital, participation size, turnover, drawdown/tail tolerance and calendar-time benchmark before comparing policies. A filter can improve mean trade return while reducing total profit; a strategy can be positive while merely loading on BTC market exposure. This is a required specification for deployment assessment, not a claim that all these fields are absent everywhere. Do not invent operator risk numbers. The current go-live bar is a useful starting constraint.

**Mechanism versus observable feature.** Order flow is real, but price impact during the measurement interval is not the same as a future return available after decision and arrival. Estimate decay starting after availability, at horizons supported by the feed. If the apparent edge vanishes before orders could arrive, better indicator extraction cannot rescue that trading implementation. Exchange-specific fragmentation also makes cross-venue signals plausible hypotheses and cross-venue fill assumptions dangerous; neither warrants an automatic venue change.

**Translation/selection and power.** backtest_gap_review REPORT.md:169-179 already identifies translated rather than original-language quotes, frame disagreements and selected examples. My first roadmap underweighted this. Preserve source reconciliation only where reproducing a trader is the goal; an explicitly OURS strategy need not reproduce Tom. Small selected winning examples establish neither precision nor profitability. Collect negatives/abstentions and independent episodes. Set an economically meaningful effect before calculating power; a nonsignificant result is not equivalence, and an arbitrary 80% threshold is not proof of edge.

**Research discipline can itself become waste.** A full book/queue infrastructure rewrite and another broad audit are not prerequisites to cheaply rejecting a minute-scale hypothesis. Stage the work: data/definition integrity, gross-effect screen with predeclared economic costs, then detailed execution for survivors, then untouched validation. Conservative and optimistic cost bounds can stop an uneconomic candidate early. Corrected inference may strengthen a negative result; we must accept that possibility.
