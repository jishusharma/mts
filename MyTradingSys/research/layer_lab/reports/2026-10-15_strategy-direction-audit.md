## What research is missing, and the road forward

The central missing experiment is **incremental, executable order-flow value at a causally known location and state**. A generic divergence-at-any-location test does not answer it. Conversely, a beautiful selected chart cannot answer it either.

1. **Repair the evidence pipeline first (Claude coordinator; GPT verifies).** Reconcile the two blocked cards above and complete the project-wide trial/access ledger. Inventory actual available data by venue, instrument, time resolution, gap coverage and timestamp semantics. DONE: one agreed dependency/status table and defensible clean-data reservation, including previously inspected periods. Reuse G6 and current chart-label work; no duplicate broad audit queue.
2. **Finish one coherent BTC experiment (Claude research design; lab implementation).** Define state → location → trigger → entry → management before outcomes. On identical eligible encounters compare location/context alone against location/context plus flow; include failures, missed moves and no-flow controls. Distinguish continuation from failed-breakout reversal. Freeze one small primary family, declare OURS values, record every variation. DONE: labels/feature definitions available at decision time, full candidate table and a deterministic reconstruction check. Complete existing T3/T4 and level/cycle cards rather than building another unrelated indicator family.
3. **Measure economic feasibility before complexity (lab + data owner).** Estimate gross effect and decay at preregistered horizons compatible with the feed. Subsecond OFI needs event-level book/trade data; minute footprint hypotheses can start with existing data but cannot make queue claims. Compare expected edge with all-in execution cost; measure passive fill selection and conditional post-fill returns. DONE: net expectancy and uncertainty under plausible execution assumptions, with fee/slippage break-even and turnover. Do not fix an uneconomic setup merely by widening its stop or adding leverage.
4. **Validate inference and simulation independently (GPT audit; Claude spot-check).** Use as-of features, chronological purging/embargo for overlapping outcomes, common-episode controls and day/session clusters. State minimum detectable effect and effective sample size. Distinguish INVALID measurement, INCONCLUSIVE power, NEGATIVE economics and PROMISING exploratory effect. DONE: raw-data and simulator spot checks plus frozen hashes; one legitimately unseen validation after choices are fixed. Do not repeatedly tune against a familiar validation period.
5. **Forward shadow, then operational testnet evidence (supervisor).** Record public-market quotes, signal availability, hypothetical order arrival/fills and realized markouts. Compare backtest and shadow distributions. Testnet checks restart, risk and order lifecycle separately. DONE: reconciliation of predicted versus observable execution and a costed OOS result; promotion remains subject to the project's go-live bar.

Suggested success dashboard: clean-data exposure, complete labeled encounters, independent feature parity, net expectancy/interval, drawdown/tail loss, turnover/cost, fill rate/adverse selection and shadow/backtest discrepancy. Counting commits, new indicators or passing tests alone is not a success measure.

## Direct answer

There is no evidence here that BTC makes order-flow automation impossible. The strongest diagnosis is a broken chain between the intended idea, measured inputs, exact strategy, credible execution and independent validation. Claude-side risk is overinterpreting proxies and negative screens while leaving cross-study exposure unresolved. GPT-side risk is faithfully executing underspecified/stale cards and mistaking local mechanical verification for research validity. Both tracks need a shared economic question and acceptance evidence.

The next milestone should be one trustworthy, costed conditional experiment, not another large sweep. It may fail: profitability is the objective, not a result we can require the data to produce.
