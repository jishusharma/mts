# Method note — pivot, level and candle audit

Evidence must distinguish a level available before the decision from an extremum labelled with future bars, then compare candle sequences against equivalent encounters on the same timeframe, phase and level origin. Predictive evidence requires a frozen chronological out-of-sample BTC test with clustered uncertainty, a registered trial family, confirmation delays, failures, censoring and fees, funding and slippage; existing development data is useful for diagnostics, not fresh validation.

Search existing MTS research first; use opened primary academic/exchange sources for evidence and original practitioners only for definitions of numbered candles. Record source tier, access and limitations; this audit proposes research specifications and does not add trader parameters or change trading rules.
