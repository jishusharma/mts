Levels ladder desk

Past results on the DEV window 2022-04-01 to 2024-06-30 only. Sealed windows unread. Percent distances only, no prices.

Parameters in plain words

Idea
When price first touches a key level in a day (4h swing highs and lows, untested volume peaks, yesterday's high, low and volume peak, last week's high, round thousands), bet that it bounces.
Entry filter E1: RSI holds
Only take it if the 14-period RSI on 5-minute candles still reads above 40 for a long, below 60 for a short.
Entry S2: wait deeper
Do not buy at the touched level. Place the whole order at the next level beyond it, against the trade, and skip the idea if price never gets there.
Stop K5
Exit if price goes 0.10 % past the day's extreme so far (pushed past the deeper level if needed).
Exit X0 2R
Take profit at the first level in favour (target 1), or at twice the stop distance, whichever the rule picks.
Costs
Real Binance fees: 0.02 % for resting orders, 0.05 % for stops and market exits. No slippage.
