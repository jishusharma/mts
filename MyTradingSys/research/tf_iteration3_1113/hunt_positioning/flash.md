## TOP 5

1. **Pattern 1 — Our numbers labelled as literature/trader numbers (`0.95/0.05`, `90`, `20`, `48`, `0.12%`):**
   - In `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/positioning/funding_cvd_fade.py:70-71` and `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/FORMULAS.md:365-376`, the layer is attributed to "Memon 2026 (7y Binance)", but the authors explicitly acknowledge that Memon does *not* provide parameter thresholds. The default lookback window (`90` records), CVD divergence window (`20` bars), upper/lower funding percentiles (`hi = 0.95, lo = 0.05`), holding horizon (`48` bars = 12h), and transaction cost (`fee_rt = 0.0010, slip_rt = 0.0004` in `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/positioning/trade_costs.py:51`) are completely internal grid parameters (`OURS`), never sourced from any trader or academic paper.

2. **Pattern 2 — Named often, never coded (Open Interest, Liquidation Cascades, Sizing Weather Warning, FlowHorse):**
   - While `MyTradingSys/system/LAYERS.md:112` titles this layer family "Positioning (funding, OI, whale / FlowHorse)", Open Interest is never coded into the backtest engine (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/positioning/funding_cvd_fade.py:1-130` contains 0 lines of OI logic; `MyTradingSys/system/LAYERS.md:113` admits "OI/long-short unused as filters").
   - Krown's liquidation cascade mechanics (`P3rDSh187TE`), leverage domino thresholds (50x down to 2x), and rule to "Check the weather before you size" have zero implementation in the layer.
   - The Flow Horse is completely unbuilt: `MyTradingSys/research/fast_track_2026-09-27/CROSS_TRADER_RULEBOOK.md:121` documents that 0 of 32 FlowHorse rules were built into code, and `MyTradingSys/research/tom_transfer_2026-10-11/HOME_FIT_AUDIT_POSITIONING.md:11` verifies that processed FlowHorse extracts have 0 mentions of funding, OI, or liquidations.

3. **Pattern 3 — One word, many claims ("Whale", "Positioning", "Liquidation"):**
   - **Whale:** Used in `LAYERS.md` to imply smart-money order flow tracking. However, `MyTradingSys/research/_archive/loose_2026-10/whale_positioning_evidence_2026-09-12.md:19,46` rejected whale tracking as coincident/lagged, Fabio refers to macro on-chain MVRV holding activity (`StaphlRH8NQ [15:09]`), and Krown refers to large market-moving accounts initiating stop cascades (`8MvxK_nxO-0`, `P3rDSh187TE`). No unified whale metric was ever defined or coded.
   - **Positioning:** Conflated across three distinct concepts: (a) academic funding rate percentile mean-reversion, (b) practitioner open-interest/long-short ratios, and (c) tape-level cumulative volume delta (CVD).

4. **Pattern 4 — Method lost in translation (Macro weather filter & cascade snapback replaced by immediate bar-level fade):**
   - Krown explicitly teaches that high OI and extreme funding do *not* signal an immediate directional trade; they are a "weather warning" / "fuel gauge" advising traders to pull capital away, wait for the cascade to flush forced margin orders, and enter *after* the dust settles (`P3rDSh187TE`).
   - Our code inverts this method entirely: `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/positioning/funding_cvd_fade.py:80-99` treats the funding percentile plus a 20-bar CVD divergence as an immediate aggressive counter-trend market entry on 15m bars, right into the teeth of potential liquidation avalanches. Furthermore, forced liquidation prints (which Krown notes carry zero trader opinion) are lumped indiscriminately into taker buy/sell volume in `cvd = 2 * tbv - v` (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/positioning/funding_cvd_fade.py:48`).

5. **Pattern 5 — Transfer assumed (Continuous 24/7 crypto execution without thin-session or weekend book filters):**
   - Krown highlights that liquidation cascades and dislocated spreads predominantly strike during "thin sessions" and "weekend books" where shallow order-book liquidity causes forced market sells to chew through price (`P3rDSh187TE`).
   - Our backtest harness (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/positioning/funding_cvd_fade.py:70-130`, `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/run_t055.py:1-120`) assumes uniform 24/7/365 execution on 15m bars without conditioning on session liquidity, weekend book depth, or exchange funding settlement frequency (1h/4h/8h intervals).

## SINGLE BIGGEST HOLE

**The positioning layer is an orphaned synthetic construct that tests neither a trader's method nor its own title:** Despite being catalogued in `MyTradingSys/system/LAYERS.md:112` as "Positioning (funding, OI, whale / FlowHorse)", the layer has zero FlowHorse code (0 of 32 rules built per `MyTradingSys/research/fast_track_2026-09-27/CROSS_TRADER_RULEBOOK.md:121`), zero whale tracking (rejected in `MyTradingSys/research/_archive/loose_2026-10/whale_positioning_evidence_2026-09-12.md:46`), and zero open-interest logic (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/positioning/funding_cvd_fade.py:1-130` does not read OI). Instead, it cobbled together an unparameterized academic paper (Memon 2026) with an arbitrary 108-cell grid (`0.95/0.05`, `90`, `20`, `48h`) and an inverted execution logic: while Krown explicitly teaches that hot funding and stacked OI are a pre-trade "weather warning" to pull risk until a cascade clears (`P3rDSh187TE`), our code fires immediate bar-level fades directly into the cascade on continuous 15m bars (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/positioning/funding_cvd_fade.py:88-91`).
