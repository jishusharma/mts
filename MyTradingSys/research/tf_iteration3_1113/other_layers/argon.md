## TOP 5

1. **Our 12/18 divergence window labelled SOURCED Malkan (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/alerts/live_confluence_feed.py:48-49`):** `WINDOW_STRONG = 12` and `WINDOW_MAX = 18` are both tagged `# SOURCED (operator relay of Malkan)`, yet Malkan explicitly states in `Wvq1TZh8aF0` that regular divergence must be **3 to 10 bars** (`>10` is diluted) and in `GbXnw_tNMtw [21:54]` states **5 to 50** (or `<15`) for reverse divergence. Neither `12` nor `18` appears in any Malkan transcript.
2. **Krown detectors disabled after failed recall, leaving only 4h S8 with invented stop/breakout (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/krown/params/krown_params.json:4`, `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/krown/krown_live.py:24-25`):** Setups S1-S7 and S9-S11 are default OFF (`enabled: false`), and `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/krown/krown_context.py:3-4` notes the detectors failed recall. Only `S8` runs live on **4h** BTCUSDT (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/alerts/live_runner_cli.py:591-593`), even though Krown states Daily is crypto's gold standard (`_t_XIBrcjRk`), replaces his compression-zone stop with `STOP_BARS = 10` and `FLOOR = 0.0056`, and drops his momentum-oscillator confirmation (`kcMnnQr1VFg`).
3. **Malkan's M/W/D Nifty 500 GFS reduced to 20-bar 1h BTCUSDT + unverified 4h WaveTrend stack (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/rsi/malkan_40_60.py:24-59`, `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/rsi/malkan_level_short.py:20-26`):** Malkan's 5-Star GFS scans Nifty 500 equities using Monthly, Weekly, and Daily 14-period RSI (`-CBvFi40sIQ`) and enters on a candle-high break with a candle-low stop (`W1HrDl70Lhk_asr [9:03]`). Our live runner runs `malkan_bull`/`malkan_bear` on **1h** BTCUSDT using a 20-bar rolling regime and close color, while `malkan_level_short` adds WaveTrend and 4h BTC pivots never mentioned by Malkan.
4. **Krown's hidden divergence stripped of trend context & RSI 40-60 mis-coded (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/rsi/krown_hidden_divergence.py:11-12`, `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/FORMULAS.md:548`):** Krown explicitly defines hidden bullish divergence as an **uptrend** continuation (`INOCBmqQupc`) and says uptrend RSI holds **40-80** while range RSI holds **40-60**. Our code strips the trend filter from `krown_hidden_divergence` and codes `40-60` as `range` (`>60` bull), misclassifying uptrend pullbacks.
5. **Major named strategies completely uncoded (Bollinger Father-Son, Spinning Top 7-Star, Crypto Microstructure):** Malkan's Bollinger Bands Father-Son (`fgN6QBFY3Ss`), Spinning Top 7-Star (`XFO49JlODxI`), Class A/B/C & 3/5/7-star divergence grades, and 35%-50% partial-exit/trailing rules (`Wvq1TZh8aF0`) have zero code.
