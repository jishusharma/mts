## SINGLE BIGGEST HOLE

**Testing Indicators in Vacuum Isolation Stripped of Structural Market Context (Method Lost in Translation):**
The single biggest gap across the entire `volume_candles_ma` layer family is the total isolation of indicators from structural auction context. Both Eric Krown, Tom Vorwald, and Fabio Valentini insist that volume, candles, and moving averages possess zero independent directional edge:
- Volume provides intensity and participation, not direction (`UqAvU1loT_k`; `MyTradingSys/research/volume_2026-10-09/VOLUME_RESEARCH_REPORT_2026-10-09.md:9-12`).
- Candlesticks are merely summaries of order flow that require structural location (support/resistance levels, value area extremes) and confirmation closes beyond wick extremes (`8MvxK_nxO-0`, `oPyUif2Zrtk`).
- Moving averages follow price as lagging noise filters and are ineffective when used as entry triggers without structural S/R alignment (`vjzy_KxJ_y8`).

Yet, in our backtests:
- `volume_2026-10-09` tested raw volume spikes and percentile thresholds in a vacuum across 553 trials, finding zero edge (`MyTradingSys/research/volume_2026-10-09/VOLUME_RESEARCH_REPORT_2026-10-09.md:9-12`).
- `candle_patterns_2026-10-04` tested 18 classical candle patterns without level confluence across 108 trials, finding zero edge (`MyTradingSys/research/candle_patterns_2026-10-04/CANDLE_PATTERNS_RESEARCH_2026-10-04.md:7`).
- `setup_alpha_beta_2026-10-10` tested standalone daily SMA200/EMA7 crossovers from an unsourced chatbot handoff, finding negative performance (`MyTradingSys/research/setup_alpha_beta_2026-10-10/ALPHA_RESULTS.md:4,8`).

The trader methodology treats volume, candles, and moving averages as secondary confirmation tools applied *only* at pre-established structural boundaries (key swing zones, support/resistance, value area extremes). By testing them as standalone mechanical entry engines, the codebase systematically proved that isolated indicators fail, while entirely omitting the integrated structural method taught by the traders.
