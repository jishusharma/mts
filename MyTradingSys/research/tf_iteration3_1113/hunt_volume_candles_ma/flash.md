## TOP 5

1. **Moving Averages as Standalone Entry Engines vs Secondary Confirmation:**
   Both Krown (-NXzJs5JRks, vjzy_KxJ_y8) and Fabio emphasize that moving averages cannot predict price and that using them for standalone trade entries is the worst way to use them. Krown insists moving averages only act as secondary confirmation when aligned with structural support/resistance zones. In direct contradiction, our `ma_daily` layer (`MyTradingSys/research/setup_alpha_beta_2026-10-10/alpha_backtest.py:34-45`) stripped away all structural support/resistance zones and tested isolated MA crossovers (SMA200 + EMA7 + RSI2), producing a meaningless negative backtest based on an unsourced chatbot handoff (`MyTradingSys/research/setup_alpha_beta_2026-10-10/ALPHA_RESULTS.md:8`).

2. **Equity Calendar Transfer to 24/7 Crypto:**
   The standard moving average periods (21 EMA for 1 month, 55 EMA for 1 quarter, and 200/256 SMA for 1 year) are derived from the 5-day-per-week traditional equity calendar (-NXzJs5JRks). Our codebase (`MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/krown/params/krown_params.json:26-28` and `MyTradingSys/research/setup_alpha_beta_2026-10-10/alpha_backtest.py:15`) copies these exact numbers (21, 55, 200) onto continuous 24/7 BTC perpetual klines without adjusting for 30-day months or 365-day years, falsely assuming calendar equivalency across asset classes.

3. **Rejection of 40 Classical Candle Patterns vs Repo Testing 108 Variants:**
   Krown explicitly warns traders that only 4 major candlestick patterns matter and that 40 textbook patterns should be ignored (8MvxK_nxO-0). Despite this, our `candle_patterns` research team spent hundreds of trials running 18 classical textbook patterns across 108 combinations (`MyTradingSys/research/candle_patterns_2026-10-04/CANDLE_PATTERNS_RESEARCH_2026-10-04.md:7`), discovering that 0 of 108 had edge. The four core patterns Krown actually uses (strict engulfing, hammer/star, conviction full body, and doji) were buried under irrelevant textbook noise.

4. **Strict Structural Engulfing vs Loose Body Enclosure:**
   In 8MvxK_nxO-0, Krown gives an exacting definition of an engulfing candle: it must trade below the low of the prior candle and close above the high of the prior candle (wick-to-wick enclosure). Standard retail definitions and some internal tests (`MyTradingSys/research/candle_patterns_2026-10-04/pilot/run_p1.py:88`) evaluate engulfing as body-to-body enclosure, discarding the structural liquidity sweep of the prior candle's extremes that makes the setup work.

5. **Transfer of Order Flow Mechanics to Bitcoin:**
   Fabio Valentini states plainly on the tape that he does not use order flow on Bitcoin and uses on-chain analysis instead (Is1HvdfQm5c). Despite this explicit disclaimer from the trader, our system attempted to apply tick-level order-flow layers, footprint absorption, and bigtrade capture matrices directly to Binance BTCUSDT (`MyTradingSys/research/bigtrade_2026-10-09/BIGMOVES_REPORT.md:7`), assuming transferability where the trader himself refuses to use it.

## SINGLE BIGGEST HOLE

**Testing Indicators in Isolation Stripped of Structural Market Context (Method Lost in Translation):**
The single biggest gap across the entire `volume_candles_ma` layer family is the total isolation of indicators from structural auction context. Both Krown and Fabio insist that volume, candles, and moving averages possess zero independent directional edge:
- Volume provides intensity and participation, not direction (UqAvU1loT_k; `MyTradingSys/research/volume_2026-10-09/VOLUME_RESEARCH_REPORT_2026-10-09.md:12`).
- Candlesticks are merely summaries of order flow that require structural location (support/resistance levels) and confirmation closes beyond wick extremes (8MvxK_nxO-0).
- Moving averages follow price as lagging noise filters and are ineffective when used as entry triggers without S/R alignment (vjzy_KxJ_y8).

Yet, in our backtests:
- `volume_2026-10-09` tested raw volume spikes and percentile thresholds in a vacuum across 553 trials, finding zero edge.
- `candle_patterns_2026-10-04` tested 18 classical candle patterns without level confluence across 108 trials, finding zero edge.
- `setup_alpha_beta_2026-10-10` tested standalone daily SMA200/EMA7 crossovers from an unsourced chatbot handoff, finding negative performance.

The trader methodology treats volume, candles, and moving averages as secondary confirmation tools applied *only* at pre-established structural boundaries (key swing zones, support/resistance, value area extremes). By testing them as standalone mechanical entry engines, the codebase systematically proved that isolated indicators fail, while entirely omitting the integrated structural method taught by the traders.
