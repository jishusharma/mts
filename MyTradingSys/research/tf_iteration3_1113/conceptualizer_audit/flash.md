## TOP 5

1. **Context layer: Visual profile shape replaced by candle ladders and CVD slope (Gap Pattern 4).** Tom Vorwald (`Ld1dhjGdTCA [NO_TS]`) stresses that distinguishing trend from balance is crucial and derived from market structure and weekly profile shapes. In `concepts.json`, the implementation cites `src/main/java/datafeed/SetupCandidatesBuilder.java:286-311` and `MyTradingSys/research/tom_fabio_course_correction_2026-11-07/auction_context.py:384-440`. Our code completely omits Market Profile TPO and volume profile shape recognition, substituting an arbitrary 20-month SMA candle ladder and 1m VWAP/CVD slopes with code comments admitting thresholds have NO-SOURCE.
2. **The 70% return claim: SOURCED label on unstated number (Gap Pattern 1 & 3).** In `concepts.json` and `MyTradingSys/backtest/vp_return_to_value.py:3-12`, our codebase attributes the claim that price returns to the POC ~70% of the time as SOURCED to Tom. In `NkQeOVDTAec [NO_TS]`, Tom states only that where high volume occurs, we often see a reaction when a market returns there, mentioning 50% hit rate and 200-300 trades purely as hypothetical math. The 70% figure represents standard Value Area volume width, not a return probability. We invented a return probability rule, labeled it SOURCED, tested it on 24h UTC bars, and declared Tom failed.
3. **Trade build: Asymmetric 1:4.5 payoff destroyed by fee gate and 1-leg cap (Gap Pattern 1 & 4).** Fabio Valentini (`Is1HvdfQm5c [21:14]`) states the primary edge is to jump in with a super small stop loss and shoot for a 1 to 4.5 with 30-40% win rate and adding from profit. Our implementation in `MyTradingSys/research/tom_fabio_course_correction_2026-11-07/auction_context.py:829-875` enforces a fee-to-stop gate requiring stops >= 40 bps to clear 10 bps BTC fees, rendering Fabio's tight index stops (0.014-0.061%) impossible. Furthermore, `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/fabio/v3/manage.py:1-25` leaves multi-leg adds uncalled, restricting all tests to rigid single-leg trades.
4. **Acceptance vs rejection: Discretionary tape speed replaced by 6-bar close rule (Gap Pattern 1 & 4).** Tom Vorwald (`k0zm5NYn0IQ [NO_TS]`, `qLnQan0KGmQ [NO_TS]`) judges rejection speed by tape action and defines acceptance as structural value building beyond the level. `MyTradingSys/research/tom_fabio_course_correction_2026-11-07/auction_context.py:646-680` invents arbitrary numerical proxies: reclaim within <= 3 bars, relative volume >= 2.0, and acceptance as 6 consecutive bar closes (which equals 30 min on 5m but 6 hours on 1h bars).
5. **Session timing: NY cash session profile exists but uncalled (Gap Pattern 2 & 5).** Fabio Valentini (`Pz8f0wWW12M [29:00]`) trades US indices strictly during the New York cash session. While `MyTradingSys/research/tom_fabio_course_correction_2026-11-07/auction_context.py:355-380` implements `prior_ny_session_profile`, no backtest or census engine actually invokes it. All backtests run on uniform 24h UTC days, ignoring the cash-session liquidity concentration fundamental to the traders' edge.

## SINGLE BIGGEST HOLE

**Systematic substitution of arbitrary fixed indicators for visual auction structure, paired with unverified asset transfer.**
Across all 8 Tom & Fabio concepts in Conceptualizer, every single rule taught by the traders is an eye-read evaluation of visual auction structure (reading profile shape from weekly TPOs, observing rejection speed on the tape, identifying passive iceberg walls on footprint charts, and capturing asymmetric 1:4.5 reward with tight structural stops). In MTS code, every visual method was replaced with rigid mathematical proxies (`rel_vol >= 2.0`, `6-bar closes`, `60-bar z-scores`, `20M SMA ladders`, `0.5 * median LVN cut`), while the actual visual tools (Market Profile TPO, per-price footprint, multi-leg add pyramids) were never coded or left uncalled (`prior_ny_session_profile`, `simulate_trade_legs`). When transferred to 24/7 Bitcoin perps, the 10 bps taker fee destroyed the tiny stops required by the method, and rather than recognizing the transfer mismatch, we widened stops 5-8x to satisfy an arbitrary 40 bps fee gate, completely destroying the asymmetric payoff that makes the methodology work.
