## TOP 5

1. **Context layer: Visual profile shape replaced by candle ladders and CVD slope (Gap Pattern 4).** Tom Vorwald (`Ld1dhjGdTCA [NO_TS]`) stresses distinguishing trend vs balance from market structure and weekly profile shapes, whereas `src/main/java/datafeed/SetupCandidatesBuilder.java:286-311` and `MyTradingSys/research/tom_fabio_course_correction_2026-11-07/auction_context.py:384-440` substitute 20M SMA ladders and VWAP/CVD slopes.
2. **The 70% return claim: SOURCED label on unstated number (Gap Pattern 1 & 3).** In `NkQeOVDTAec [NO_TS]`, Tom only says high-volume zones often produce reactions; `MyTradingSys/backtest/vp_return_to_value.py:3-12` labels a 70% return probability as SOURCED.
3. **Trade build: Asymmetric 1:4.5 payoff blocked by fee gate and 1-leg cap (Gap Pattern 1 & 4).** Fabio Valentini (`Is1HvdfQm5c [21:14]`) targets 1:4.5 R:R with a super small stop and adds from profit, whereas `MyTradingSys/research/tom_fabio_course_correction_2026-11-07/auction_context.py:829-875` enforces >= 40 bps stops and `MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/fabio/v3/manage.py:1-25` leaves multi-leg adds uncalled.
4. **Acceptance vs rejection: Tape speed replaced by 6-bar close rule (Gap Pattern 1 & 4).** Tom Vorwald (`k0zm5NYn0IQ [NO_TS]`, `qLnQan0KGmQ [NO_TS]`) reads tape speed and structural acceptance, whereas `MyTradingSys/research/tom_fabio_course_correction_2026-11-07/auction_context.py:646-680` hardcodes `<= 3` bars reclaim and `6` consecutive closes.
5. **Session timing: NY cash session profile uncalled (Gap Pattern 2 & 5).** Fabio Valentini (`Pz8f0wWW12M [29:00]`) trades strictly in the NY cash session, while `prior_ny_session_profile` in `MyTradingSys/research/tom_fabio_course_correction_2026-11-07/auction_context.py:355-380` is never called by backtests.

## SINGLE BIGGEST HOLE

**Systematic substitution of rigid numeric proxies for visual auction structure, paired with unverified transfer from index futures to 24/7 BTC perps.**
Every core rule in `concepts.json` is an eye-read of profile shape, tape speed, or footprint absorption, yet the codebase replaces them with uncalibrated thresholds (`6` closes, `3` bars, `60`-bar z-scores, `0.5 * median` LVN cut) and widens stops to clear 10 bps BTC taker fees, destroying the asymmetric payoff.
