## Card 1: wt30-lower-high-divergence-short (the operator's setup)
Operator words (Bible): after the up-move from the lower band to the red bands, WT tries to come down to the centre but does not, three waves, then it comes down through 0, goes up again, price is higher than the previous high, WT lower: divergence; the green line coming down confirms; "the only trade which is the best trade". His example: 11-02 ~03:00-04:30 short.
Event (OURS, 30m): a down-cross (wt1 crosses below wt2) with wt1 in 0..53 (the confirmation); an earlier down-cross within 48 h with wt1 >= 45 and at least 10 higher; wt1 went below 0 in between; the max high over the bars from the dip bar (the bar of min wt1 between the two crosses) to the confirming bar exceeds the max high over +-k bars around the earlier cross, k = 3 bars (OURS). Entry at the confirming bar's close, short, exit after 24 bars (12 h) or never earlier (OURS; the exit is not his, no stop is claimed).
His-context subset (OURS): an up-cross at <= -53 within 8 days before, and >= 3 down-crosses with min(wt1,wt2) > 0 since that up-cross (the 'waves above 0'; the count 3 is his word, SOURCED Bible).
Question the study answers: what separates his chosen examples from the many weak events. Features (each OURS, each a cell, counted in m): size of the WT drop below 0 between the two crosses; hours since the last 30m valid top dot; session (08/13/21 UTC cutoffs); RSI14 lower at the second high (his d-context-retest-1104 rule); 4h wt1 slope.
Bin edges (OURS), frozen here before any VALIDATION read: drop size = DEV-event terciles of the wt1 minimum between the crosses (3 bins; edges computed on DEV only); hours since last top dot 0-12, 12-24, >24 (3); session by UTC cutoffs 08/13/21 = 21-08, 08-13, 13-21 (3); RSI14 at the second high lower vs not lower than at the first (2); 4h wt1 slope sign up/down (2).
m = 14 (marginal cells) = 3+3+3+2+2 = 13 feature bins + 1 his-context subset, not the full grid, feeding `prereg_gate.py --m 14`.
Metric: net bp after 0.14 % at 24 bars, first-touch -1 % vs +1 %, with the unconditional event and a same-window placebo (random 30m bars, same count) as controls. Candidate only, so far (coordinator scratch, not pre-registered, overlapping events, read bars past DEV_END): n=1219 net -6.1 bp; his context n=975 -3.4 bp; 2020-22 +8.9 bp, 2023-26 -12.0 bp. Those are NOT inputs and not a prior; the PRE_REG must not be tuned to them.
Verdict rule (OURS, as every study): a cell PASSES only if Holm-adjusted net CI > 0 on DEV and a confirmation on data not seen in line 5 confirms the sign and CI. A BTCUSDT-only VALIDATION 2024-07..2025-06 read is not a clean confirmation (line 5: already read) and is exploratory; the PRE_REG must name the unpeeked confirmation target (a different instrument over VALIDATION, or a later forward window) before any run. ## Card 2: wt-mtf-cycle-nesting
Question: does a 5m/15m valid zone dot, traded only in the direction of the 30m cycle phase and the 4h slope, beat the same dots traded without nesting?
Phase (OURS, from the Bible): 30m phase UP if the last 30m valid dot is a bottom, DOWN if it is a top; age = hours since that dot divided by the median 30m cycle length measured on DEV (unit = "median cycles"); 4h slope = sign of wt1 now minus wt1 three 4h bars ago. Entry: a 5m or 15m valid bottom dot long when 30m phase UP and 4h slope up; valid top dot short when phase DOWN and 4h slope down. Exit 12 bars of the entry TF. Arms: nested; unnested baseline (same TF, same dots, no gate); phase-only; slope-only; age bins 0-0.5, 0.5-1, >1 median cycles. Primary read (OURS) per TF: mean net bp of the nested trades vs mean net bp of the baseline dots the nesting gate rejects (nested trades are a subset of the baseline with identical returns, so a pairwise difference is undefined); statistic = difference of the two means, 95 % day-block bootstrap CI (block = one UTC day, resampling days; zero-trade days in an arm contribute no trades and stay in the resample); counts n_nested and n_rejected stated per TF. Fee-net. No result is a trading claim.
