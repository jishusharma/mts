## Hypothesis
The RSI 40/60 line-behaviour study (rsi_40_60_line_behaviour_2026-11-04/RESULT.md) found one consistent effect: when another timeframe's RSI sits at the same line, the hold rate rises by 7 (1h), 12 (4h), 18 (12h) and 17 (1d) points, and when the higher-timeframe bias agrees with the bounce, the bounce holds 10-14 points LESS. Never traded. T11: the confluence bounce. T12: the confluence bounce against the HTF bias.
