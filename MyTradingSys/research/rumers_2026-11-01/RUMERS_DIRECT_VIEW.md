## 3. Ranked Candidate List (Complete Entry & Exit for DEV Backtest)

The 7 candidate rules below have complete, unambiguous entry, stop loss, and target conditions. They are ranked in order of mechanical precision, reproducibility on historical OHLCV bar data (e.g. Binance BTC 1m aggregated to 5m/15m/1D), and amenability to pre-registration under `HARNESS.md` [OURS]:

### Rank 1: `RUMERS-ORB-MANIP-REVERSAL` (Videos: `6WfTIyJ-YzQ` R03/R04, `T0H_jpQXdVI` R20, `aTMFouI9mF4` R17)
- **Timeframe:** 15m session anchor + 5m execution.
- **Filter:** Opening 15-minute bar range >= 0.20 * Daily ATR(14) [SOURCED: `6WfTIyJ-YzQ` ~08:00].
- **Trigger:** Reversal candle on 5m (hammer / inverted hammer or engulfing / 30-50% retracement) after 15m manipulation bar. Enter on next 5m bar breaking the signal bar.
- **Stop Loss:** Strictly beyond session high of day (for Short) or session low of day (for Long).
- **Target:** 50% midpoint of the 15m opening range for TP1; 100% (opposite boundary of 15m box) for TP2.
- **Why Rank 1:** Highest mechanical clarity. Both filter (20% ATR threshold) and exits (session extreme stop, 50% midpoint target) are strictly mathematical without subjective chart reading.

### Rank 2: `RUMERS-BOX-PDH-PDL-FADE` (Videos: `EoQDQVblmMY` R07, `pcYO-yzAtrQ` R23)
- **Timeframe:** Daily reference levels + 5m execution.
- **Filter:** Identify Previous Day High (PDH) and Previous Day Low (PDL) and range midpoint.
- **Trigger:** Price pierces beyond PDH or PDL during the morning session, then prints a 5m rejection candle that closes back inside the previous day range. Enter fade trade on close of that 5m bar.
- **Stop Loss:** Beyond the highest high (fade short) or lowest low (fade long) of the sweep wick.
- **Target:** 50% equilibrium midpoint of yesterday range for TP1; opposite boundary (PDL for short, PDH for long) for TP2.
- **Why Rank 2:** Fully mechanical on daily and 5m bars. Directly aligns with existing MTS level sweep concepts (`layers/levels`), making backtest implementation straightforward.

### Rank 3: `RUMERS-EMA9-PULLBACK-TREND` (Videos: `xtN0U-j_4Jc` R29/R30, `l0j1irTKIUw` R34)
- **Timeframe:** 5-minute.
- **Filter:** Trend bias established by price holding above 9 EMA (bullish) or below 9 EMA (bearish). Prohibit entries if price is overextended (> 1.5 ATR from 9 EMA).
- **Trigger:** Price pulls back to touch the 9 EMA line and prints a rejection candle in trend direction. Enter on close of the rejection candle.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed just below the pullback swing low (Long) or above pullback swing high (Short), or 0.1 ATR beyond 9 EMA.
- **Target:** Fixed 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio or dynamic trailing stop along the 9 EMA until an opposite close occurs.
- **Why Rank 3:** Standard, highly testable moving average pullback model. Yields high sample size (n >= 300) over 27 months of DEV BTC data.

### Rank 4: `RUMERS-VWAP-BOUNCE` (Video: `kozesVBKkg4` R14)
- **Timeframe:** 5-minute.
- **Filter:** Trend bias dictated by price relative to intraday VWAP (above = long only, below = short only).
- **Trigger:** Price pulls back to touch or test the VWAP line during regular session and prints a bullish hammer / engulfing (for Long) or bearish rejection (for Short). Enter on candle close.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed just below the local swing low formed during the VWAP test.
- **Target:** Previous session high/low or fixed 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio.
- **Why Rank 4:** Clean institutional benchmark setup. Requires session volume data for VWAP calculation, which is available in Binance 1m klines.

### Rank 5: `RUMERS-EMA9-SEPARATOR-RETEST` (Video: `l0j1irTKIUw` R34)
- **Timeframe:** 5-minute.
- **Filter:** Momentum surge creates a separator condition (distance to 9 EMA >= 2.0 * ATR_5m).
- **Trigger:** Following the separator surge, price has its first orderly pullback to test the 9 EMA line without closing below it. Enter continuation on the first bullish 5m close.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed below the low of the retest pullback bar.
- **Target:** Minimum 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio or prior swing high.
- **Why Rank 5:** Targets high-velocity breakout continuation, but lower event frequency than regular EMA pullback.

### Rank 6: `RUMERS-RSI-STRUCTURAL-DIVERGENCE` (Video: `hMURCHLnZVE` R27)
- **Timeframe:** 15m structural levels + 5m RSI (14).
- **Filter:** Price tests a major predefined structural level (PDH, PDL, or 15m fractal swing level).
- **Trigger:** At the structural level, RSI prints divergence or hooks out of extreme territory (< 30 for Long, > 70 for Short) accompanied by a 5m reversal candle.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed beyond the extreme price wick at the structural level.
- **Target:** RSI 50 midline (TP1) and opposite structural boundary (TP2, minimum 1:2 R).
- **Why Rank 6:** Excellent theoretical risk-to-reward, but requires joint occurrence of structural level touch and RSI extreme/divergence, resulting in moderate sample size.

### Rank 7: `RUMERS-UNSHARP-3CANDLE-EXEC` (Video: `N6zlfBJsoRc` R11/R12)
- **Timeframe:** 5-minute.
- **Filter:** Price approaches a key structural pivot or box boundary.
- **Trigger:** Three-candle sequence: Candle 1 pushes into level, Candle 2 prints rejection wick / pause, Candle 3 breaks Candle 2 extreme in reverse direction. Enter on break of Candle 2 extreme.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed just beyond the extreme of Candle 2.
- **Target:** 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio, with immediate emergency exit if next bar closes back through Candle 2 body.
- **Why Rank 7:** Tight invalidation and rapid execution, but candlestick pattern definition needs strict numerical parameterization for wick-to-body ratios in `PRE_REG`.
