### Q29 What counts as proof, volume shape, touch count, delta with confirmation

**Verdict: ANSWER**
Proof is a multi-step sequence rather than a single bar: Tom requires a reaction at a predefined high-volume level (e.g., repeated excessive buying and balance), while Fabio requires a three-step order-flow sequence (absorption, rewarded aggression in the opposite direction, and re-absorption of the trapped side). Volume shape signals are falling volume under a level, a volume spike that then trades back down/below the spike (exhaustion), a dry-up in volume on a pullback, or high volume printing only a wick without breaking the level (failed effort). Tested levels weaken with each attack (Fabio notes 3rd/4th test in live narration, Tom checks off tested levels as higher probability to fall through, with no fixed numeric touch limit stated), and delta divergence is never traded alone—it must be confirmed with footprint/order-book absorption, candle result, or market speed.

### Q30 What he reads in the book while in a trade

**Verdict: ANSWER**
While in a trade, Tom reads **market speed** and **liquidity** in the order book to manage partial sequences: he stays in while liquidity trades quickly and aggressively in his direction, steps out when the book stalls ("nothing is happening anymore") and counter-side sellers push in, and expects continuation when liquidity thins and speed picks up again. Guest Serge Hoffmann reads resting limit-order size (whether big orders get eaten or reloaded), time-and-sales speed (high-speed repeated absorption fails; slowing absorption holds), and exits counter-trades as soon as speed picks up against them—noting that swing traders only use the book for entry and do not watch it during the hold. Fabio watches speed of tape, passive liquidity walls stacking/reloading, and book thickness (Deep Effort) while trailing behind big trades or moving to break-even when volatility spikes; no numeric speed threshold is stated.

> "as long as liquidity is being traded quickly and aggressively in your direction and you see the surplus is there you stay in. Now all of a sudden you see in the book that nothing is happening anymore. Suddenly the seller starts pushing into the market. Then you know okay I'll stay out until suddenly again when there is a lack of liquidity and the market speed picks up for you again then you know the probability is quite high that we'll eventually reach the top."
**Source GPS: 3o1VVsfBmmU | END** (Tom)

> "if you see absorption happening at a very high speed, and you can see that it's being absorbed again and again and the speed is stable in that sense, then the probability is very high that the absorption won't hold. But if you see absorption and notice that things are slowing down, the whole level is slowing and absorption is taking place, then the market might even peak above it for all I care. The main thing is that you don't see any aggressive buyers there."
