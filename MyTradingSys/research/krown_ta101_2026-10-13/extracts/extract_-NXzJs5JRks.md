### K--NXzJs5JRks-05 SMA versus EMA
- Type: INDICATOR
- Stated mechanics: A simple moving average is the plain average of the closes. An exponential moving average weights the most recent closes more, so it is more responsive and turns faster, which means it catches real turns sooner but is also faked out sooner. Both have their place; EMA is his favourite. no numbers given
- Quotes:
