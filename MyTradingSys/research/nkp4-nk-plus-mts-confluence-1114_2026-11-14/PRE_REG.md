## Rules (9; NK rule definitions frozen from NK6, source NK extracts research/nk_stocktalk_1113)
NK layers as long/short 15m booleans (K=4-bar persistence for event layers 1,2,3,5): N1 first-hour low/high break and reclaim (UTC 00-01 OURS), N2 standard pivots P/R1/S1 cross (S: NK 3EpXq8JC4jU), N3 breakout retest/fakeout (concept S, numbers O), N4 Hilega-Milega RSI9/EMA3/WMA21 state, N5 RSI14 30/70 re-cross (S).
NN = at least 2 NK layers agree. Confluence rules (the point of this study): X1/X2/X3 = at least 1 NK layer active AND at least k of the 6 MTS exhaustion-in-direction states agree (long: C1 LOW, C2 OS, C3 LOW, C4 UNWIND, C5 NEG, C6 BULL-DIV; short: the mirror bucket; mapping OURS). Count of NK + MTS agreeing layers in the phase direction is therefore explicit in X1-X3, and every context bucket is also tested one at a time as a cell so the data, not the mapping, picks the useful buckets.
Fire on the onset bar (rule true now, false on previous bar), per coin per direction, no overlapping trades. Signals are picked over all phases and tagged with phase and context; phase-appropriate direction = phase table direction (ACCUM: geometry).

## Context layers (one layer per cell, 3 buckets, constants fixed now)
C1 4h RSI14 <=40/mid/>=60 (S Malkan 40/60); C2 daily WaveTrend LazyBear 10/21 <=-53/mid/>=+53 (S: LazyBear defaults); C3 4h Bollinger %B (SMA20, 2 pop sd) <0.10/mid/>0.90 (period S, cutoffs O); C4 OI 24h change <=-3%/flat/>=+3% (O); C5 mean of last 3 funding settlements <0/0..0.03%/>0.03% (O); C6 24h taker delta vs 24h price (bull-div P24<0 & TD24>0 / agree / bear-div; O; fresh test at 24h); C7 RV7/RV60 <0.7/mid/>1.3 (O); C8 UTC session Asia 21-07 / EU 07-13 / US 13-21 (O). 4h, daily and 1h values use the last COMPLETE bar at the signal bar close. Cells per rule: 6 x 8 x 3 = 144; family = 9 rules x 144 = 1296 cells, of which at most 27 are kept.

## Trade management (OURS, as NK6)
Entry next 15m open. Stop S = max(1.0 x ATR14, 0.5%) (>= max(0.6 ATR, 0.5%)); target 2R; time exit 24 bars; stop+target same bar = stop; a gap through the stop fills at the open when worse. Fee 12 bp round trip taker primary (net R = gross R - 12e-4 x entry / S); maker row 6 bp with a fill model: limit at signal close, filled only if the next bar trades through it, unfilled trades dropped.

## Method (spec s3)
Step A (per-phase table first, DEV; the same table is repeated on VAL after the freeze, descriptive only): per rule x phase x direction n, days, gross R, cost R, net R at 12 bp; per rule x phase the phase-appropriate direction vs the opposite one (day-clustered difference and se). Report this before any cell results; if no rule shows appropriate > opposite, Step B is labelled exploratory.
Step B (DEV only): cell needs n>=150 and >=40 distinct days; score = lower 90% bound (5th percentile of 2000 day-cluster bootstrap draws, seed fixed) of mean net R at 12 bp; keep top 3 per rule, only if score>0, the phase carries >=5% of DEV days and the direction is phase-appropriate; at most one kept cell per context layer; zero kept is valid. `cells_frozen.json` (with the git hash of the study code) is committed before VAL.
Step C (VAL, once, kept cells only; run.py val refuses unless cells_frozen.json is committed and *.py unchanged since the hash it names): n, days, win%, gross R, cost R, net R, day-cluster 95% CI, ex-best-5-days net R, 6 bp maker row, placebo (1000 random entries from the same cell matched on direction and hour; real minus placebo and p), BH at q=0.10 over kept cells (one-sided day-cluster bootstrap p). PASS = net R>0 at 12 bp, CI low>0, ex-best-5>0, placebo p<0.05, BH q<0.10, n>=150; n<150 = UNDERPOWERED; else FAIL. Controls per kept cell: opposite direction in the same phase/bucket, same bucket in the other 5 phases, phase-appropriate rule over all buckets, unconditional rule (all phases, both directions). Also reported: how many DEV cells passed VAL, DEV net vs VAL net (shrinkage).
No phase-pooled headline: every result is per phase. "Useful trades now" section lists, descriptively, which kept cell is active for BTC and ETH given today's phase label and current context buckets.
