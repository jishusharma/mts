# WaveTrend outside MTS: source-led setup inventory

Verified 2026-10-17. This is an independent external-source pass; no MTS implementation or backtest was inspected. The purpose is to define distinct, falsifiable WaveTrend models, not to claim a profitable BTC perpetual strategy.

## Method note, fixed before searching

Effectiveness would require a reproducible rule set tested on a dated BTC perpetual sample with signal timestamps, trade count, chronology-preserving development/validation/untouched holdout, and realistic maker/taker fees, funding, spread, slippage and stop execution. The setup must survive net-of-cost, side-specific and regime-specific tests, multiple-trial correction and plausible parameter perturbations. Indicator pages, screenshots, social reactions and in-sample Strategy Tester panels can define candidates but cannot establish an edge.

Source tiers were fixed before results: T1 peer-reviewed/institutional study with method; T2 preprint/exchange documentation or fully replicable practitioner study; T3 original author or practitioner script/description for definitions; REJECT unattributed/inaccessible or return claims lacking costs and sample. No T1/T2 validation of a WaveTrend trading edge was found in this finite pass. Exchange and platform documentation below are T2 for mechanics, not WaveTrend performance.

## Original definition and what the author actually says

[LazyBear's 2014 TradingView publication](https://www.tradingview.com/script/2KE8wTuF-Indicator-WaveTrend-Oscillator-WT/) calls WaveTrend a port of an earlier TS/MT indicator. The author proposes a sell when the oscillator crosses **down** through its signal line above an overbought band, and a buy when it crosses **up** below an oversold band; the page explicitly says the marked crosses are not its only potentially useful signals. It does not specify BTC, perpetuals, stops, exits, or measured win rate. **T3 | definition only | high confidence for author intent, no confidence in predictive edge.**

An [archived Pine copy attributing the code to LazyBear](https://gist.github.com/marketcalls/f9c795c7d5ba0f988761) gives the standard defaults: channel length 10, average length 21, upper bands +53/+60, lower bands -53/-60, source HLC3, and a four-bar SMA signal. The computation is `esa=EMA(HLC3,10)`, `d=EMA(abs(HLC3-esa),10)`, `ci=(HLC3-esa)/(0.015*d)`, `WT1=EMA(ci,21)`, `WT2=SMA(WT1,4)`. This is a third-party mirror of attributed author code, so compare it against the live TradingView source before implementation. Zero-deviation warmup behavior and EMA seeding also need a written convention. **T3 | code definition only | medium confidence in provenance.**

## Parameters and failure modes

The original 10/21/4 ±53/±60 is the baseline. The pages expose materially different changes: ClassicScott's 55/6 two-speed pair (and 14 alternate), Maydo's 9/12 with volume, and thomcam's adjustable thresholds. Changing channel/average periods changes both lag and distribution of WT values; a fixed ±60 band need not retain the same event frequency. Compare event rate, crossing delay, and threshold occupancy by market regime before interpreting returns. Do not search many lengths/levels against one holdout; pre-register a small candidate set and account for all trials. [Original attributed code](https://gist.github.com/marketcalls/f9c795c7d5ba0f988761), [ClassicScott](https://www.tradingview.com/script/Quc7HH7r-WaveTrend-Oscillator/), [Maydo](https://www.tradingview.com/script/JtAecR07-Market-Sniper/).

Failure mechanisms to measure: repeated countertrend extreme crosses during a trend; mid-zone chop producing rapid crosses; late confirmation from pivot divergence or smoothed/higher-timeframe series; repaint-like visual hindsight when a divergence marker is drawn on an earlier pivot; isolated volume spikes around UTC/funding events; and too-short stops whose gross move is smaller than round-trip execution costs. These are test risks inferred from rule mechanics, **not measured WaveTrend failure rates**. A public [Python gist of a WaveTrend backtest](https://gist.github.com/binhna/7f3f30e10ff0c823ac13ea3ecde9f88a) is useful as a negative example: it enumerates stop/target percentages and computes hit fractions, but the opened code uses spot CSVs, shows no dated chronological holdout, fee/funding/slippage model, or same-bar tie policy. T3 code, not performance evidence.

For perps, funding is a position cash flow and can change frequency with contract conditions; the [Binance primary FAQ](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/360033525031) documents scheduled payments and interval changes. This supports accounting mechanics only; venue-specific rate histories and actual contract fee schedules are needed for any BTC test. TradingView's [strategy documentation](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/strategies/) explains hypothetical broker-emulator fills and commission/slippage settings, and its [nonstandard-chart warning](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000481029-strategy-produces-unrealistic-results-on-non-standard-chart-types-heikin-ashi-renko-etc/) explains why synthetic Heikin Ashi prices can yield unrealistic strategy results. Both T2 for platform behavior.

## Community signal, carefully bounded

Popularity is visible, but it is not a survey of outcomes. A [2023 Reddit TradingView discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/TradingView/comments/180yozi/what_is_your_absolute_favorite_community_script/) contains one recommendation of LazyBear WaveTrend and cites its likes; that is one favorable anecdote, not a review count or validation. Derivative authors repeatedly add trend, divergence, volume or higher-timeframe context, suggesting practitioners do not regard a raw cross as a complete trade plan; this is an **inference from the sampled publications**, not a community-wide sentiment statistic. [ClassicScott](https://www.tradingview.com/script/Quc7HH7r-WaveTrend-Oscillator/), [AdonisWerther](https://www.tradingview.com/script/xkkyJgCO-On-chart-Wavetrend-Divergence-with-Pivots/), [jadamcraig](https://www.tradingview.com/script/Tts2YhVP-Wave-Trend-w-VWMA-overlay/).
