## Audit dispositions

**C1 definition/implementation mismatch — confirmed, fix before another C1 outcome read.** The frozen definition requires a confirmed price pivot paired with a confirmed WT pivot. `count_events.py` compares WT values at price-pivot locations without confirming WT pivots. The synthetic reproduction still prints price pivots `[(10, 12), (20, 22)]`, WT pivots `[(12, 14), (18, 20)]`, and a reported C1 event `[(22, 1)]`. The compared WT values are not WT pivots. Keep the historical atlas intact; a corrected detector needs a fail-first fixture, independent review and a newly frozen count-only/preregistration version before any new outcome calculation.

**Control-arm uncertainty — limits inference, not a detected edge.** The event arm uses day-clustered uncertainty, while the saved random/context controls use dispersion across 200 draw means and `diff_test` combines them as independent. That does not preserve common market-day dependence. No cell passed the existing screen; this gap does not establish a hidden positive result. Before another outcome run, preregister and review a matched day/block bootstrap or randomization comparison and freeze the analysis version.

**Input content pinning — prerequisite to reproducibility.** Existing exposure entries name the klines directory and analysis source but do not pin the exact input files by digest or record duplicate/missing timestamp checks. Before another outcome read, record exact DEV filenames, sizes, hashes, timestamp coverage, duplicate and missing-minute counts, and usable bars per timeframe; bind that manifest to a new ledger entry. Preserve the existing ledger row.

**Verdict wording correction — metadata only.** The already-read Fable re-gate Q3 confirmed the nine-cell set under its revised 200-event-day floor and required W04–W08 to be `INCONCLUSIVE`; W01–W03 and W09 stay `FAIL`. `VERDICTS.csv` now follows that instruction with cost `0.14` and `one_run=yes`.
