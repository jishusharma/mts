# T055 backtest - C3 (turn_of_candle + quarter-hour opening order imbalance)

Verdict: **NO EDGE (FAIL)** - same rule as C1/C2 (OOS n>=30, net mean>0, net Sharpe>=0.3, PBO<0.5, same IS/OOS sign, >=2 positive OOS years). No horizon passes any of the net-mean, Sharpe or year criteria.

## Source (verified 2026-09-29)
Kim & Hansen, "The Quarter-Hour Effect: Periodic Algorithmic Trading and Return Predictability in Cryptocurrency Futures", arXiv 2607.09426 (10 Jul 2026, v2 16 Jul). Abstract: "opening order imbalance predicts returns over four to twelve hours". Six Binance perps, trade-level data. Tier T2 (preprint, no replication, cost model not in abstract). The abstract does not give the sign of the relation, so momentum and contrarian are both IS-grid variants.

## Limits of the proxy and test
- Paper uses trade-level data on six perps; we have BTCUSDT only and 1m-kline taker-buy volume (no aggTrades on disk, no trade-size roundness, no exact quarter-hour-open sub-minute window). The paper's imbalance may be finer than one minute and cross-sectional over six assets; a null here on BTC-only 1m proxy does not refute the paper, it only says the tradable BTC version on our data has no net edge.
- Data: 2022-01..2026 months on disk; funding only from 2025-09 (earlier trades fee+slippage only, as in C1/C2). At 4-12h holds funding is at most 1-2 records, negligible versus 12 bps cost.
- Multiple horizons/variants were tried; none passed, so no multiplicity adjustment needed for the null.

## Recommendation
No alert-only live sampling warranted for C3 (no gross edge at 4/8/12h on BTC proxy). If the operator wants forward confirmation anyway: alert-only log of opening-minute taker imbalance with 4h/8h/12h forward returns, and only after real aggTrades data is available for a faithful replication. Never a Tom filter (layer stays separate).
