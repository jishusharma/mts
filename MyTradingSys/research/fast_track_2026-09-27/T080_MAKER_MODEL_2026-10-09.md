# T080 maker execution model: verdict (2026-10-09)

Task T080 (BUGS.md). Question: how much of the fee drag does limit entry + limit target + taker stop remove, and is any cell net R > 0
with the CI above zero. Tom core trades, DEV 2025-09-01..2026-06-25 only (HOLDOUT burned, not read). Tables below the marker are
generated by `python MyTradingSys/backtest/maker_model.py` (all cells printed, none dropped); this section is hand-written.

## Verdict (plain)

1. **The maker structure removes about half of the fee drag, and that is all it can do.** Overall (watch_episode, 520 filled trades)
   mean fee_R falls from 9.325 (A, all-taker) to 4.654 (B): -4.671 R, 50.1 %. Per bucket the cut is also 50 % (0.381 of 0.747 at
   0.10-0.20 %, 0.188 of 0.375 at 0.20-0.40 %, 0.087 of 0.174 at >=0.40 %). It is exactly half because the 5 bp entry fee disappears while the
   exit stays taker: only 7 of the 520 fills (1.3 %) exit at the target, so the maker target leg saves almost nothing.
2. **No cell has net R > 0, and none has a CI above zero.** Best overall cell: B/C watch_episode net R -4.947 [-11.231, -1.643]; best
   bucket: 0.20-0.40 % B net R -0.265 [-0.617, +0.157]. The p4_episode cells (n = 3 to 18) have CIs that straddle zero on the upside
   but every mean is negative (best -0.186 [-1.374, +1.340], B, 0.10-0.20 %, n = 7); that is noise-level, not evidence.
3. **The reason is gross R, not fees.** Gross R (before any fee) is -0.294 [-0.455, -0.108] over the 520 filled watch_episode trades:
   significantly negative at zero fee. Fees (even at maker 0) only deepen a loss that already exists. Zero-fee B would still lose.
   The tiny-stop trades (<0.10 %, 309 of 520 fills) carry fee_R of 7.6 R (B) or 15.3 R (A) per trade because R is a few bp of price,
   so they dominate every mean; the medians (net R -1.5 B, -2.0 A) show the typical trade loses about 1 R plus fee.
4. **Fill realism (C) changes nothing measurable.** Requiring a 1-tick (0.5 USD) trade-through lowers neither fill rate (93.7 % in B
   and C) nor ran-away rate (0.7 %); net R is -4.948 vs -4.947. 0.5 USD is 0.0004-0.0008 % of the 60.8k-124.6k price range of the
   fills, far below a 1m bar's range, so a touch and a 1-tick through are almost the same event on 1m data (the results differ
   on 7 of the 555 signals). This does NOT validate the maker fill assumption: queue
   position and partial fills are invisible on 1m bars and are the real risk (see limits).
5. **Adverse selection (D) is small at the P1 value and visible at 1 bp.** D_0.02bp adds 0.019 R per filled trade (net -4.966);
   D_1bp adds 0.934 R (net -5.882). Even charging 1 bp, the maker structure keeps 3.74 R of the 4.67 R fee saving overall
   (cost diff vs A, D_1bp). In buckets >=0.10 % the 1 bp charge is 0.02-0.08 R against a 0.09-0.38 R saving, so the saving survives.
   The 1 bp cell is a stress, not a measurement.

Bottom line: maker execution is worth building on a candidate that already has positive gross R (it halves the hurdle), but it does
not rescue Tom core as simulated: gross R is negative, so net R is negative in every regime, bucket and variant.
