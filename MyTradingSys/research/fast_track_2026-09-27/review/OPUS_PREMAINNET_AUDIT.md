## Verified OK (riskiest items checked, found sound)

- Env selection: testnet is the default. Mainnet needs flag + `.secrets/mainnet.secret.json` env=="mainnet" + typed input(), and the secret env must match in both directions. auto_trader has no path to --mainnet, and its subprocesses stay on testnet. MTS_ACCOUNT is refused on mainnet, so orders cannot go to a wrong account (R2); the side effect is #2.
- Mainnet 50 USD per-order cap runs before any HTTP call, BTC-PERPETUAL only. Reduce-only orders (stop, targets, re-placed stop) are exempt and cannot add exposure. Only the stop uses edit() (reduce-only).
- Units: BTC-PERPETUAL amount/size are USD. round_size floors to 10 USD steps and never rounds up. risk_guard and the executor size checks use the same unit.
- Ambiguous outcomes: order-creating calls never auto-retry on transport errors or 10050 (OrderStateUnknown). Only 10028/10047 and the 13009-11 re-auth resend, and those are pre-processing rejects.
- Stop: placed on the first fill and resized on every new fill. HALT never blocks the stop. fail_closed/flatten_halt order: cancel entries+targets by id AND label -> close -> re-read flat -> cancel stop last. A flatten failure leaves the stop and HALT in place.
- Deribit semantics: close_position is automatically reduce-only (docs). A reduce-only oversize order clamps and does not reverse (testnet G1b). Edit keeps reduce_only (testnet G1a: reconciler, which requires `reduce_only` on the live stop, returned reconciled_ok after a pm edit). get_open_orders_by_instrument defaults to type `all`.
- position_manager: a sign flip or an unreadable/absent stop flattens within MAX_UNPROTECTED_SECONDS. A growth halt keeps a stop that covers the new size. Flat means leftovers are cancelled.
- Clock: MAX_UNPROTECTED uses monotonic(). No request signing depends on the local clock. The trade-lookup window has +60 s / -3600 s slack.
