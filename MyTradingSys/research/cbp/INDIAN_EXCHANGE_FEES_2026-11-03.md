Tax (Budget 2026-27, unchanged): 30 % on VDA gains, no loss offset; 1 % TDS on every sale; 18 % GST on fees. Sources: decrypt.co/356601, kucoin.com news flash, outlookmoney.com.

Read: on an INR-native exchange a retail round trip costs about 0.9 % plus GST before TDS; on Binance about 0.2 %. The 1 % TDS per sale is the biggest friction on any venue and dwarfs the exchange-fee gap. Not advice; the operator chooses the venue.
