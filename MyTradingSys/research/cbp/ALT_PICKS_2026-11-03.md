## 2. What the research says (three web reads, 11-03; unverified by design, operator's instruction)

- Starts after BTC dominance peaks and breaks down, never before. 2017: dominance 87 % to 33 %; 2021: 70 % to 40 %. Runs 5 to 11 months.
- Today: dominance ~60 %, at the 2026 high, no break. CMC altseason index 59, Blockchaincenter 45 (it read 11-12 at the June low: extreme bitcoin season, the contrarian reading he quotes, "greedy when fearful").
- Survivorship: of the top 100 in Dec 2016, 9 were still top 100 in 2024. Of 26 that stayed top 100 from 2017 to 2021, 21 slid down. The 2021 winners are now 94 to 100 % below peak (the shared artifact). So "buy the last cycle's winners" is the losing rule; each cycle's 10x names are new, from the new narrative, with small caps at the start.
- Narratives named for the next leg: stablecoin rails, RWA / tokenization, perp DEXes (share of all perps 2 % to 10 %), AI agents, privacy (ZEC already ran), DePIN.
- Pump and dump: low float lists, pumps, insiders and VCs sell into it, unlock cliffs add supply. Checks: FDV/mcap (> 2 is a warning), unlock calendar, vol/mcap spikes, top-holder share.

18.6-year land cycle (Harrison / Anderson, as Jason Pizzino uses it):
- 14 up, 4 down. Phases: recovery, mid-cycle slowdown (2019-20), winner's curse (2023-26), land peak (~2026), equities crash 1 to 2 years after the land peak (2027-29), trough ~2030.
- Pizzino, Aug-Oct 2026: "latter stages", stocks still have further to go, BTC must break ~83k then the ~120k prior peak; Google Trends BTC 14 (no retail), stablecoin dominance 8.9 % (risk zone 11-12 %), alts must hold their bases, ZEC an early warning. He does not say whether the July low (~57.7k) is THE low.
- BTC in the macro crash: March 2020 fell ~50 % in two days with stocks, recovered faster; 2022 topped Nov 2021 before the S&P (Jan 2022) and fell 75 %. BTC tops with or before stocks, falls harder. Critique: the cycle only clearly held 1990-2016, small sample.

The exit rule matters more than the entry: van de Poppe's own rule, "the deeper into the bull, the less in alts".
