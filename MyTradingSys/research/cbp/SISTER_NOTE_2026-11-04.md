- Property and markets move in an 18-year cycle (Harrison, Anderson; Jason Pizzino applies it to crypto). Crash 2008 (Lehman), recovery 2012 to 2019, mid-cycle dip 2020, final boom 2023 to 2026. Property peaks around now. Stocks and crypto make their last big run AFTER that, 2027 to 2029, then the next crash.
- Bitcoin also has its own 4-year cycle. Last low was late 2022, peak 2025, and the bear of 2026 is the one we are in. Ben Cowen (the numbers guy): Bitcoin dominance goes to 60 to 66% before alts get their turn, and the bottom was due around October. Jason Pizzino: Bitcoin must get back above 83,000, then the old high near 120,000; he watches stablecoin share, above 11% means danger. Michael van de Poppe (the alt guy): buy alts by DCA, slowly, when nobody is talking about them, and hold less of them the deeper the bull goes.

Bitcoin itself is only 53% down (last time 77%). So the alt bear is further along than the Bitcoin bear. That is why the plan is Bitcoin first (you have it), alts now in slow slices.
