**2. The cycle-bottom logic, in plain words (why we think the low is near or already in)**
Think of three clocks on the wall. We buy alts when two of them say "near the bottom"; we sell when they say "near the top".
- Clock 1, the calendar. Bitcoin has topped and bottomed on a four-year rhythm for 16 years. From each top, the bottom came 363 to 406 days later. The last top was 6 Oct 2025 at 126,200. 363 to 406 days later is 4 Oct to 16 Nov 2026. We are inside that window right now, with 9 days left.
- Clock 2, the depth. Past bears fell 85 %, 84 %, 78 %. Each one shallower. This one fell 54 % so far, to 57,800 on 1 July. The shape of that low (a second low slightly under the first, five months apart) is the same shape as the 2022 bottom. Alts have already fallen 70 to 94 %, which is a full bear for them.
- Clock 3, the long averages. At every past bottom, Bitcoin dipped below its 200-week average price and then climbed back over it. In July it dipped 8 % below (that average is 67,800 today); it is now 11 % above. Same pattern as before.
- Two of the three clocks say "July was the bottom". The calendar says "a last dip can still come this month". That is exactly why the plan is three phases and not one lump: if July was it, phase 1 was enough; if a last dip comes, phases 2 and 3 buy it cheaper.
- What would prove us wrong: a week closing under 60,000, or a new low under 57,800. Then we stop buying and wait, no arguments.

**4. How to buy, step by step (CoinSwitch, spot)**
1. Money in: UPI or IMPS into the app wallet in INR. Deposit is free; keep a screenshot.
2. Open the coin, choose "Limit", not "Market". A market order buys at whatever price is on screen, often 0.5 to 1 % worse. A limit order waits for your price.
3. Price: today's price minus 2 to 3 % for phase 1, minus 5 to 8 % for phase 2. Amount: the fixed total you wrote for that coin, divided by three phases.
4. Place it and leave it. Orders can sit for days. Check once a day, not every hour.
5. Write every buy in a note: date, coin, quantity, INR paid. You need this for tax (30 % on profit, 1 % TDS on every sale, losses do not offset gains, so we sell rarely).
6. Bitcoin itself: same steps. You already hold it from the 7x run; add only in phase 2 or 3 and only the amount you wrote down.
7. Never: futures, leverage, "earn" or lending products, coins tipped in a group. Never buy because a candle is green.
