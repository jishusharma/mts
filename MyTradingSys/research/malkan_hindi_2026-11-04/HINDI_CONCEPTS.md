    - Plain meaning (OURS): The bar count between the two swing points must strictly be less than 15 bars; counts of 20, 25, 50 bars cause the divergence to dilute.

    - Plain meaning (OURS): The fewer the number of bars spanning the divergence, the more powerful it is. Minimum is 3 bars; beyond 10 bars (15, 20, 25, 50 bars) it dilutes.
