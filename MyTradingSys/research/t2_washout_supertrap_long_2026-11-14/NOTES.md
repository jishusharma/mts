# NOTES: reading choices (coder, step A). Rules are untouched; each line is an ambiguity resolved closest to the PRE_REG text.

- Grid and gaps: 1h bars sit on a full UTC hour grid, missing bars are NaN. RSI, ATR and BB restart inside each contiguous run, so no indicator value uses data across a gap. A real trade whose bars entry..entry+24 are not all present is dropped as "unresolved" (also data end); a signal window that meets a missing bar returns no signal.
- Cache layout: core 15m files use keys t or ts (ALGO uses ts), fresh 5m files use one array `a` (t,o,h,l,c,v); checked in code (ms timestamps, median spacing, high >= low).
- Trap time columns are bar OPEN times (UTC). Trap day for the phase_daily lookup = UTC day of the trap candle's open time; split DEV/VAL by trap open time < 2024-01-01.
- "Next 12 bars after the trap": signal bars are trap+1 .. trap+12 (the trap candle itself cannot signal). VWAP is anchored at the trap candle (included) through the current bar.
- BTC 4h gate: 4h bar starting s is usable iff s + 4h <= trap close (trap open + 1h); so the 4h bar ending exactly at the trap close counts. RSI seed per FORMULAS.md (SMA of first 14 deltas); BTC 4h RSI is NaN (no trap) until 15 contiguous 4h bars exist.
- One position: after a trap with no signal the coin is free again from bar trap+13; after a signal the coin is free from the signal bar+1 if the trade is skipped (D cap, unresolved), else from the exit bar (a new trap may be the exit bar itself). A trap while armed or in position is ignored (first trap wins), counted nowhere.
- Skips: D > 10% of entry (245 signals), unresolved (146), no signal (638), no entry bar (1). ATR NaN never occurred. Skipped signals are not trades and not in episodes.
- Stop: low <= stop counts as hit (stop price fill); open <= stop is a gap fill at the open; checked bars entry .. entry+23; exit bar entry+24 exits at its open.
- Episodes: built over all trades of all coins (core and fresh together) sorted by signal bar open time, CHAINED: a new episode starts when the gap to the previous signal is more than 72h (PRE_REG gives both "within 72h of the first" and "gap of more than 72h"; the gap rule was taken, it contains the first reading). Episode cycle = cycle of the first trade (earliest signal, ties by coin). Column episode_cycle added to trades.csv.
- Placebo (stats.py, not yet run): candidate bars per (coin, cycle of the bar's UTC day, DEV/VAL of the bar time), entry bar not inside any real position of that coin (entry..exit bar of any real trade) and bars entry..entry+24 present; no washout gate on placebo bars. Real trade's D/entry is the stop fraction; fee term = 0.0012 / stop fraction. Placebo episodes reuse the real trade's episode grouping; a draw mean = mean over episodes of the mean of its placebo trades; the matched cycle is the trade's own trap-day cycle.
- Holm: one-sided bootstrap p (share of resample means <= 0, plus-one corrected), Holm over the 2 cells, flagged if adjusted p > 0.025 (matches the 95% two-sided CI). OURS.
- Fresh-8 bar: episodes rebuilt from the cell's fresh trades only (chained 72h on their own signals).
- Top-coin removal: the coin with the largest summed fee-net R in the cell is removed; episodes rebuilt from the remaining trades.
- Buy-and-hold basket: equal weight over the coins in trades.csv; yearly = first to last 1h close in the year (coins with > 30 days in that year); per state = hourly log returns on bars whose UTC day is in the state.
- Mean per-trade pct return in stats = R_net12 x D / entry.
