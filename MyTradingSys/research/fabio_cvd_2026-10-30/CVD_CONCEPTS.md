## 1. What delta and CVD are (his definitions)

- **Delta**, SOURCED: "Delta is the difference between aggressive market participants on the buy side and aggressive market participants on the sell side." [Pz8f0wWW12M]
- **CVD**, SOURCED: "the cumulative volume delta that is the summary of all the delta exchange in the session" [DyS79Eb92Ug]. He resets it per session.
- **How he uses it**, SOURCED: as a pressure gauge and a "leading volume indicator" [0jM5Y31YJak]. In his words, "the volume is leading the price" [0jM5Y31YJak].
- **Two views:** horizontal delta (the delta footprint or profile, by price) and vertical delta (per candle, and the CVD line) [Pz8f0wWW12M].
