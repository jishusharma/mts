# Quick levels / sizing probe — frozen before results, 2026-10-14

Question: does staged position building and disposal improve a simple BTC level-rejection trade at equal maximum planned loss? Compare eight fixed arms on DEV December 2024–March 2025, paired by signal day; report costs, drawdown and day-bootstrap uncertainty. This is exploratory, not LC1–LC4 completion or an out-of-sample edge claim; April–June stays unread.

All parameters below are OURS, not attributed to Tom or Fabio. Source education defines sizing and partial exits, not their profitability. Web search preceded this written method note; record that procedural deviation rather than backdating it.

- Prior full UTC day's high/low, POC and contiguous 70% value area from actual footprint bins. Greedily expand from POC toward the larger adjacent bin (lower wins ties). Levels frozen at midnight.
- First minute rejection of a level: previous close on trade side, minute touches level, closes back on that side within 0.1%. Next-minute market entry. First eligible signal per day, long or short, no simultaneous campaigns.
- Stop 0.4% beyond rejected level; nearest opposing listed level must be at least 0.4% from signal close and next open. Second target is the next listed level or twice first-target distance when absent. No bias/RSI filter in this quick isolation test; not a faithful complete Tom/Fabio implementation.
- Entry arms: single; equal 1:1:1 adds on adverse moves of 0.25/0.5 initial stop distances; capped 1:2:3 at those adverse thresholds; 1:2:3 adds on favorable moves of 0.25/0.5 initial stop distances. Adds require a completed minute and execute next open, at most one per bar. No adds after first partial exit. Never widen stop.
- Exit arms: all at first opposing level; or 50% at first, 25% of original held quantity at second, remaining 25% on adverse five-minute net delta or opposite Bollinger band (20,2 population standard deviation), after first target. Signal exits at next open. Close all at final daily close. No breakeven-stop variant.
- Fixed reference equity $10,000, campaign loss budget $50 (0.5%), including modeled execution costs to stop. Quantity weights normalized by planned weighted stop losses; each actual add clipped against remaining budget and 2x reference-equity notional cap. Fractional linear BTC quantities, no lot rounding. This is a linear synthetic execution study, not a Deribit inverse-contract simulator.
- Taker fee 5bp plus adverse slippage 1bp on each fill (combined cash charge 6bp). Funding stress charge 1bp of held notional per eight elapsed hours, accrued per minute; it is NOT historical funding. Stop fills at worse of stop/open; stop precedes targets in ambiguous bars. Minute data cannot recover intrabar path or depth. No liquidation engine, mark-price model or cross-collateral simulation; no leverage safety claim.
- Descriptive monthly and aggregate results; 2,000 paired day-bootstrap samples, seed 42, mean difference against single/full exit. No selection or rerun tuning based on results. Eight trials, all disclosed. No random-level control, so no conclusion that these levels beat chance.

Sources to verify: CME Proper Position Size (stop distance and risk budget); Schwab How Traders Can Take Advantage of Volatile Markets (partial exits); Deribit Fees and Liquidations (execution costs and margin constraints). These support mechanics, not expected returns.
