## GO / SKIP per axis (primary variant `watch_episode`)

- **S (session): SKIP.** Rule: a 0-2h / 2-4h session window beats all other trades with a Bonferroni CI above 0 (n >= 100). Windows tested: 8; passing: 0.
- **Q (selection): SKIP.** Rule: any selection split (first per session / day, proof level, tier) has a Bonferroni CI of the gross R difference excluding 0. Comparisons: 2; excluding 0: 0.
- **M (management): GO.** Rule: GO iff MFE >= 1R before the stop in >= 25% of trades and >= 2R in >= 10%. Observed 40.0% and 27.4%.
- Consistency check on `p4_episode` (n = 59): S SKIP, Q SKIP, M GO.

## Reading (hand-written for the 2026-10-11 run; the tables are regenerated on rerun, this text is not)

- **M = GO, with a caveat that limits what it proves.** 40.0 % of trades reach 1R and 27.4 % reach 2R inside 4 h before touching the stop, and 27.6 % of the STOPPED trades had already been 1R in profit (15.3 % at 2R). So a break-even stop or a partial at 1R would change the outcome of about a quarter of the losers. But 1R is only about 0.084 % of price (about 57 USD at 68k), minute-scale noise on BTC: the median 4 h MAE is 5.7R and the median trade is stopped, so the same wiggle that gives the MFE also kills the trade. Ignoring the stop, 85 % of trades go 1R and 65 % go 3R within 4 h, i.e. the stop sits inside the noise. M therefore says "there is room for management on the existing entries", not "the entries have edge"; any M arm must be judged net of fees (about 2.8R per stopped or timeout trade at these stops) and together with the stop-width F arms.
- **S = SKIP.** No 0-2h or 2-4h window beats the other trades (best: US_open 0-2h, +0.22R difference, Bonferroni CI [-0.41, +1.01]). The pooled 0-2h and 2-4h buckets are negative (-0.20R and -0.40R) and the "outside every window" bucket is the best (+0.08R, carried by 26 target hits, CI includes 0). This is the opposite of "the edge lives at the open". The null column is too noisy to read per bucket (100 draws of a heavy-tailed outcome: DAX/London 2-4h shows +0.90).
- **Q = SKIP, but the test is thin.** Only two comparisons qualified (n >= 100 on both sides): first-per-session vs rest (-0.04R) and first-per-UTC-day vs rest (+0.16R); neither excludes 0. "First per session" holds 79.5 % of trades, so it is almost the whole sample and cannot separate anything. Proof count has only levels 3 (n = 2641) and 4 (n = 58, mean -0.57R, worse, below the n >= 100 gate); tier has one value (`trigger_ready`). The history has no book-imbalance/funding/OI proofs, so proof-based selection (Q2) cannot be tested on this data; Q3-Q5 (market state, HTF bias, max trades per day) need features the rows do not carry and were not tested.
- **Level, as in the earlier run:** mean gross R -0.165 [-0.302, -0.001] before fees, median -1.0R, 70 target hits in 2699 trades; dev period -0.29 [-0.46, -0.11].
- **p4_episode (n = 59)** is too small for any axis.
- Not done, not claimed: no session-matched null (the helper randomises time inside the UTC day), no fee view (gross R only, as asked), no Q3-Q5 features.
- Spot check: one stopped trade (2024-03-09 17:00Z long) had its pre-stop 4 h MFE recomputed straight from the raw kline CSV: 1.5836R, equal to the module value.
