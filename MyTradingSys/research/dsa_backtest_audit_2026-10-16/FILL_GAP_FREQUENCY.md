# FILL_GAP_FREQUENCY: how often a stop meets a gap-through bar (follow-up to FILL_CANARY)

Measurement only, no engine edit. DEV 2022-01-01..2024-06-30 only (no VALIDATION/HOLDOUT bar read). Generated by `fill_gap_frequency.py`; test `test_fill_gap_frequency.py` (planted 3-bar series: share 1/1, understatement 0.99 R = (99.00 - 98.01) / 1.00).

Gap-through = bar open beyond the stop in force. Understatement = |stop - open| / D (R), what a fill at the stop price hides.

## Engine fill rules (file:line)
| Engine | Rule | file:line |
|---|---|---|
| fabio v3 walk_trade | fill bar: exit at the stop price even if the open is beyond it (F scenario, optimistic); later bars: exit at the open when gapped | MyTradingSys/backtest/layers/fabio/v3/manage.py:140-143 |
| sim.py --stops ideal | every stop exit at the stop price s (optimistic on every gap); observed = snapshot px | MyTradingSys/backtest/sim.py:68-69 |

## Result
| Population | n stop exits | n gap-through | share | mean R understatement (gapped) | p95 R (gapped) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proxy, all stop exits | 10252 | 12 | 0.117% | 0.280 | 0.690 |
| post-fill-bar (sim ideal misprices these; v3 already at open) | 10141 | 12 | 0.118% | 0.280 | 0.690 |
| fill bar (v3 F scenario misprices these) | 111 | 0 | 0.000% | 0 | 0 |

Per stop exit (gapped or not) the mean understatement is 0.00033 R.

## What was and was not measured
- v3 on real DEV order-flow bars: NOT_IN_DATA. `F:/MTS/data/orderflow_1m` starts 2024-12-02 (0 day files inside DEV), `binance_raw/aggTrades` is empty. The v3 engine cannot generate its own signals on DEV.
- Stand-in (OURS, labelled): v3's real `walk_trade` stop logic (BE move, S8 exhaustion switched off at runtime because plain bars lack order-flow fields; no file edited) on 1,313,200 BTCUSDT SPOT 1m bars (`F:/MTS/data/bigtrade/spot_raw`, DEV months only, spot not Deribit). Entries: a resting limit at the previous close every 240 bars, both sides, stop 0.3% away (v3 cell f0.003), filled on the first later bar touching the limit, 1440-bar horizon (longer trades are not stop exits). 10,928 entries, 10,252 stop exits. Entry timing is not v3's signal timing, so the share is a bar-structure rate, not v3's trade rate; a real signal set clustered at news/volatility could be higher.
- sim.py ideal on DEV snapshots: NOT_IN_DATA. `Dumps/backtest/replay_rows.json` covers 2026-09-13..2026-09-25 (8,993 rows), outside DEV.

## Reading
On liquid BTC 1m bars a stop is gapped about 1 in 850 exits, mean 0.28 R when it happens, so the stop-price convention understates average stop-exit loss by ~0.0003 R per stop: negligible versus the 2 bps fee scale. It matters only for tails (p95 0.69 R) and for illiquid or event-driven entries. A shared fill module is not justified by this number (scope_out); re-run on v3 signal exits once DEV-period order-flow bars exist.
