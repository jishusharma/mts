## Indicator Settings

### RSI Configuration
- **Indicator:** Relative Strength Index (RSI)
- **Period:** 14 (RSI 14)
- **Source:** Close prices (standard)
- **Applied to:** Intraday, Daily, Weekly, Monthly timeframes

### Zone Approach (Unconventional - replaces traditional 70-30)
Instead of traditional overbought (70) / oversold (30) levels, Malkan uses:

**Primary Range:** 40-60 zone represents consolidation/sideways movement  
**Key Inflection Points:** 40 (support in bullish), 60 (resistance in bearish)

Source: "When the market is bearish, it won't be able to cross 60, and whenever in a bullish trend, it won't be able to cross 40."  
URL: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/

### Range Shift Concept
Malkan defines RSI range differently per market regime:

| Market Regime | RSI Range | Lower Bound | Upper Bound |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bullish Trend | 40-80 | 40 (support) | 80 (resistance) |
| Bearish Trend | 30-65 | 30 (floor) | 60 (resistance) |

Source: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/  
Quote: "Range Shift is a phenomenon that occurs when RSI shifts from one range to another due to price changes."

### Trend Definition via RSI
- **Bullish Market:** RSI cannot drop below 40; when it bounces from 40, trend is strong upward
- **Bearish Market:** RSI cannot rise above 60; when it falls from 60, trend is strong downward
- **Consolidation:** Price trapped between 40-60 RSI = sideways/low-probability setup

## Named Setups

### Setup 1: 5-Star RSI Strategy (Grandfather, Father, Son)

**Also known as:** Grandfather Father Son strategy, Multi-Timeframe RSI Confluence

**Timeframe Alignment:**
- Grandfather = Monthly RSI
- Father = Weekly RSI
- Son = Daily RSI

#### Bullish 5-Star Setup

**Confirmation Criteria:**
1. Monthly RSI ≥ 60 (grandfather bullish)
2. Weekly RSI ≥ 60 (father bullish)
3. Daily RSI ≤ 40 (son at support level)

**Entry Trigger:**
- Green (bullish) candle forms at or near Daily RSI 40 level
- "Entry point is determined by placing an order above the high of the signal candle"

Source: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/  
Quote: "Bullish candlestick forms at 40 RSI level on daily chart = buy signal."

**Stop-Loss:**
- Place below the low of the bullish entry candle
- Source: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/

**Target:**
- First Target: Daily RSI level 60
- "Target is 60 RSI level on daily chart"
- Source: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/

**Trade Management:**
- Position held as long as Monthly & Weekly RSI remain > 60
- Invalidation: Monthly or Weekly RSI drops below 60

#### Bearish 5-Star Setup

**Confirmation Criteria:**
1. Monthly RSI ≤ 40 (grandfather bearish)
2. Weekly RSI ≤ 40 (father bearish)
3. Daily RSI ≥ 60 (son at resistance level)

**Entry Trigger:**
- Red (bearish) candle forms at or near Daily RSI 60 level
- "Bearish candlestick forms at 60 RSI level on daily chart = short signal."

**Stop-Loss:**
- Place above the high of the bearish entry candle
- Source: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/

**Target:**
- First Target: Daily RSI level 40
- Quote: "Target is 40 RSI level on daily chart"
- Source: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/

**Evidence of Use:**

### Setup 3: 3X3 Swing Trading Framework

**Core Concept:** Three pillars across three candle patterns

**Three Pillars:**
1. **Price Action** (candle patterns)
2. **Momentum** (RSI divergence, volatility)
3. **Volatility** (Bollinger Bands, Range Shift)

**Named Sub-Setups within 3X3:**

#### 3.1 - RSI Divergence (Positive & Negative)
- **Positive Divergence:** Price makes lower low while RSI makes higher high (bullish reversal signal)
- **Negative Divergence:** Price makes higher high while RSI makes lower low (bearish reversal signal)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cQ-y1oULkBw (Divergence Trading Strategy by Vishal B Malkan)

## Filters & Management

### Multi-Timeframe Filtering
**Primary Filter:** Monthly & Weekly RSI acts as gate for Daily entry

Quote: "When Monthly and Weekly RSI is above 60, if RSI takes support at 40 in daily chart, it bounces back. If bullish candle forms at 40 RSI level, enter the stock."

Source: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/

This is the core engine: higher timeframes confirm trend direction; daily provides entry timing.

### Price Action Filters
- Entry candle color (green for long, red for short) at inflection points (40 or 60)
- Candle size/momentum at entry level signals commitment

[INFERRED] Based on: "A bullish candlestick forms at the 40 RSI level" language in sources.

### Stop-Loss Placement & Risk Management
- **Long SL:** Below entry candle's low (high-low = risk per trade)
- **Short SL:** Above entry candle's high

Source: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/

**Risk-Reward:** [GAP] No explicit R:R ratio stated; Target (60 RSI or 40 RSI) distance varies per stock price.

### Entry Trigger Refinement
- "Entry on green candle" = specific candlestick that closes above 40 (for long)
- "Above the high of the signal candle" = limit/market order placement

Source: https://blog.elearnmarkets.com/a-5-star-rsi-strategy/

### Trade Invalidation Rules
**For Longs:**
- If Monthly RSI drops below 60 → exit or reduce position
- If Daily RSI closes and sustains below 40 without reversal = signal failure

**For Shorts:**
- If Monthly RSI rises above 40 → exit
- If Daily RSI closes and sustains above 60 without reversal = signal failure

[INFERRED] From: "Confirmation Requirements must be maintained for trade viability."
