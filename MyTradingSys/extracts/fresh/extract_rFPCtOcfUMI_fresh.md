## KEY FINDINGS

1. Early reversals can be identified by absorption in the order book: large passive buying/selling on one side.
2. Footprint chart shows market sell/buy orders; large volume at top with zero below indicates strong accumulation and possible reversal.
3. When large trades happen on the trader's side, it indicates strong demand accepting prices, not completed auction.
4. A selling tail is several PBOS stacked on top of each other and can signal a major reversal.
5. A B profile is the first sign of strong, aggressive selling.
6. A single print at the high that moves back into the previous value area is a good reversal sign.
7. The 80% rule says probability is high that market returns to the previous value area.
8. Cumulative delta divergence alone should not be used; it must be confirmed with footprint or order book.
9. Support and resistance form only when active aggressive trading occurs at a level, not from a drawn line.
10. Volume spike at highs followed by slowdown and aggressive sellers supports a reversal trade.
