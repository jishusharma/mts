## VIDEO CONTEXT

Tom argues against chasing one "best setup." He says traders fail because they lack clarity about whether a setup is reproducible across market phases. He presents a process-oriented path: use the right tools, fit the strategy to the trader's personality via "trader DNA," implement discipline and suitable risk management, and work within a team for feedback. He presents this as more important than testing thousands of setups.

6. Risk management must suit the trader and be tailored to the strategy.
