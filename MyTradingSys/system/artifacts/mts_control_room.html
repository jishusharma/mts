How this page works

  1. The repo is the only place work is tracked: the registry (BUGS.md), the study findings and the trade ledger. Nobody edits this page by hand.
  2. A script rebuilds the page from those files every day, so it always matches the repo.
  3. You have two inputs. Decisions answer what Claude asks you. The Inbox takes anything you want to tell Claude, word for word. Claude reads both, acts and replies under each item.
