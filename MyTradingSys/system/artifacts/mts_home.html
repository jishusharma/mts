How to use MTS

What do I do today? Answer anything under Waiting for you. Send chart screenshots to Eagle_Eyes. A backlog idea: paste You are Admin_Guy. plus the idea (recipe 6). If any login's meter says SPEND (Pipeliner shows all of them), start one super session (recipe 3). That is all: nothing runs by itself.

Open a new session in the Claude Code app (model Sonnet), paste the line, add your words. Any role starts on any machine and any login; the "where" below is only what the role needs. The first reply must start with ROLE LOADED; if it does not, close that session and open a new one.

  1. Ask a question or make a decision Any time, any machine, any login. Paste You are Admin_Guy. then your question (Haiku, high effort; it records your words and passes judgement on as a card). the answer from the repo, your decision written to the record, and what still waits on you.
  2. Read a chart screenshot During market hours. On JIS-NB it reads the live files; on any other device it reads a copy marked mirror@time and says so. Paste You are Eagle_Eyes. then the screenshot. a logged read against the engine, tagged NOTE or LAB-VERIFY, and a one-line flag if live money looks wrong.
  3. Spend spare budget: one super session When the weekly meter says SPEND. Read the weekly bar in the Claude app's usage view (or ask any session for get_usage): SPEND = the weekly % used is 10+ points below the share of the week already gone (e.g. 40% used on day 4 of 7, 57% gone), or under 85% with less than a day to the reset. Any device and any login with budget works; ask Start the Pipeliner. in any session for the SPEND state of every login. Paste the line below with your login letter and tasks. It starts the Supervisor, which runs the Session_Planner and up to 4 workers itself: do not open those by hand. hours of work on your goal, each ship checked on the trunk, and one line at the end.
