The bazaar

Narrator's picture

A merchant shouts 90 for saffron and nobody buys. At 80 a few buy. At 70 a crowd forms and trade goes on all afternoon. At 60 the sellers go home. A market is an auction looking for the price where the most business gets done. Price moves fast through prices nobody wants and sits still where everybody trades.

"where the market is in balance, everyone is satisfied. And where the market is out of balance, there is often dissatisfaction among the big players."Tom, TOM-030

The two moods: balance and trend

"when supply and demand are balanced, the market will always try to return to that equilibrium."Tom, TOM-031
"this is the case in about 70% of market phases"Tom, TOM-044. His own statistic, not verified.
  • Fast trend: almost no pullbacks, leaves fair value gaps that often get revisited (TOM-048, TOM-050).
  • Normal trend: steady with pullbacks, stays on one side of VWAP. "If the price is below it, they're initially more bearishly inclined." (TOM-049)
  • Slow creeping: grinds along (TOM-050).
  • Rising, flat or falling weekly POCs show where the big money moves (TOM-196). Weekly POC went 83896 to 86510 in the week of 2026-10-05: rising. Ours: MARKET_ANALYSIS_2026-10-05
4

The market cycle

BALANCE --(pressure builds)--> BREAK OUT OF VALUE --(acceleration)--> TREND
   ^                                                                    |
   +---------- new BALANCE at a new price <--(exhaustion, B/P shape)----+
  • Break: "Very often it accelerates at first ... a lot of market participants fall out of balance and then they sell their positions" (TOM-186).
  • A B-shaped profile is "the first sign ... that there has definitely been strong and aggressive selling" (TOM-036).
  • A failed break "came back in, formed acceptance" (TOM-011): one of the best stories in the method.
  • Right after a big move the market is sorting itself out and the action is WAIT. Ours: setup_type_rules.md section 3

Tom's way: Idea, Proof, Math

"I need an idea for a route plan and I need proof that this idea should be put into action right now."Tom, TOM-135
  • Idea: go from one volume node to the next. "I expect a reaction first. And a reaction is usually a counter at first." (TOM-134) In balance: "I can definitely look for short trades up here and long trades down here." (TOM-042)
  • Proof: rejection wick, absorption, finished auctions ("only when auctions are completely finished", TOM-013), speed ("the speed with which it rejected this is promising", TOM-010).
  • Math: the stop goes below the last liquidity; the target is the next node.
"even with a hit rate of 30 to 40% you have an average riskreward ratio of greater than three and you will be incredibly successful"Tom, TOM-091
"through your hit rate and the minimum usable risk-reward ratio, you can know, 'I have to trade at least up to here, otherwise the whole thing makes no sense.'"Tom, TOM-093

Fabio's way: listening to the engine room

Source: research/fast_track_2026-09-27/FABIO_TRADE_MODEL.md (FTM).

  • Balance first. No direction, so wait.
  • Hidden pressure. Price flat while CVD runs one way: the leading signal.
  • Effort vs result. Big aggression, no progress: absorption. Aggression that moves price: control. The trigger is the first bar where pressure gets a result, with big trades behind it.
  • Tiny stop just past the absorbed extreme; breakeven when price explodes.
  • Add on each new big-trade confirmation. Trail behind the last level where his side showed control, not bars or ATR. Exit when big trades stop pushing.
  • Pick the day: volatile, directional days; the NY cash open.
  • Payoff: many scratches and a few winners of 1:5 to 1:19.

Tom asks where they will react. Fabio asks who is winning right now. In MTS they stay separate layers.

Fast moves: what the traders actually say

Do they guide each other?

Ours: code audit of 2026-10-12

No. They run side by side and never read or gate each other.

  • Fabio v3 may import only stdlib, orderflow_bars and its own siblings (tests/test_b127_fabio_v3_isolation.py:1-8).
  • Tom core is Java; no Java file reads the layer alerts. The consensus module only observes: "never gates, vetoes or delays a core" (consensus/hook.py:8-9).
  • Layers never filter Tom (test_layer_boundaries.py:3-9). A Fabio-gates-Tom wire needs an operator ruling first.
  • In the live runner a Tom trade and a Fabio trade can be open at once on different accounts, same or opposite sides, unaware of each other (auto_trader.py:719-775).
  • The traders themselves overlap: Tom uses absorption as proof (TOM-025); Fabio uses value area and profile. Neither names the other. Recommendation: do not wire; measure agreement as a descriptive tag.
