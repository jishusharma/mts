# PRACTITIONER TOPICS - what BTC options traders actually talk about (web search 2026-10-09, T3: ideas only)
Read = search-result text, 2026-10-09. No Reddit/X/Discord thread was opened (not reachable), so this is press, exchange-desk and vendor commentary, not trader forums.
Nothing here is evidence of profit. Every item is a hypothesis for the backtest queue (OPTIONS_PLAN Question A).

1. Cash-secured put selling / wheel (put, assigned, then covered call). A Deribit executive (via boorghani.com RSS mirrors) described rising stablecoin-secured put selling as accumulation; put/call ratio spikes are partly CSPs, so P/C is a noisy sentiment gauge.
2. Covered-call overwriting by holders: selling higher-strike calls for premium, said to weigh on DVOL.
3. Expiry-day positioning: max pain, OI concentration by strike ($80k / $60k zones in one report), "pinning". Deribit itself noted recent quarterly expiries did not consistently pin settlement. Max pain figures differ by source and date.
4. Dealer gamma (GEX): long-gamma dealers dampen realized vol, short-gamma amplify; used as a regime label. Needs OI by strike, which our trade-print data does not have.
5. Skew: 25-delta put-over-call premium across short tenors (GreeksLive via press) used as a sentiment/hedging-demand gauge.
6. Listed-ETF options: IBIT expirations Mon/Wed/Fri from 2026-02-02; daily-call income funds (BITK, Tuttle); BITA premium-income ETF launched 2026-06-16 (25-35 percent overwritten). FalconX: IBIT about 50 percent of IBIT+Deribit notional end Q2 2026, about 43 percent by 2026-07-24, IBIT more put-heavy; Deribit favoured for calls. Implication to test: income-seller supply may compress call IV; US-hours flows.
7. Delta-neutral / carry (spot long, perp short, funding, basis) and its risks: funding flips negative, basis compresses, margin and depeg shocks (CoinMarketCap Academy, bit.com).
8. Short-strangle blow-up lore: uncapped short call, ignoring Greeks (OptionSellers 2018, commodities, not crypto). Test R1 tail loss and margin, not only the mean.
9. Weekend / short-dated selling (our R1) and 0DTE-style daily income: marketed as consistent; unverified.
