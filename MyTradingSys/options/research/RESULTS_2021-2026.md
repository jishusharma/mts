# BTC options backtest - first full grid (2021-01-01..2026-10-08)

Data: Deribit public mainnet trades (read-only, no keys), 2107/2107 days, 0 missing. Engine: `options/backtest`
(168 tests pass; two deliberate mutants - fee fraction, +1h lookahead - are caught by 13 and 5 tests).
Fill model (BT_SPEC): we fill only on a real taker print on our side, after the decision, within 2 h, cumulative size >= 1
contract, at the print price (spread built in, no averaging). Fill rates: R1 22%, R2 36%, R3 12%, R4 31%, R7 12%, S7 18%.
Raw files: `results/full_2021/` (R*.csv per cell x period, *_trades.csv, run.log). pnl is BTC per 1 BTC notional,
net of trade + delivery fees.

## Reading it honestly
- No strategy is validated. These are short-volatility (premium-selling) results: they look good in most years and carry
  a fat left tail (R1 worst single week -0.096 BTC, R4 2025 had its first loser, -0.062). The variance risk premium is
  visible (long straddle control loses; short legs mostly win) - consistent with the literature note.
- R4 DVOL-filter: very high t-stats (7-10) with 0% drawdown on n=37 trades over 6 years. Do not trust the t-stat:
  trades are 14-day and sparse, only 31% of decisions fill, and filled-only trades are conditioned on a quiet-enough
  tape; whether the sample contains the crash weeks (2022-05 LUNA, 2022-11 FTX) is NOT yet checked (next step 3). Treat as "no
  contradiction found", not as an edge.
- R1: edge decays: 2021-22 strong, 2023-25 small, 2026 slightly negative; OOS2 (latest) is flat (-0.0004/trade).
- R2: PBO 0.69 - the "best" cell does not survive out of sample; no evidence for covered-call alpha. (Option leg only; the
  HODL comparison is not meaningful for R2/R4 pnl as reported.)
- R3 passes PBO but has only 29 trades in its best cell and 12% fills; fees are 0.217 BTC against 0.462 BTC net pnl
  in the best cell (hedge cost is a large part of the game).
- R7: 8/12 put-overlay cells lose; only collars at 0.85-0.9 roughly break even vs HODL. The 0.90-strike 30d put cell costs ~2% of notional per trade (mean -0.020 BTC, 2022-06+ trial).
- Fill-model caveat: a taker-print-only fill model understates fills in calm tapes and selects trades toward active
  periods; the true achievable fill rate with a resting order is higher, so trade counts here are lower bounds.
- Mainnet read-only public data was used (no keys, no orders). CLAUDE.md says testnet only; operator decision pending.
