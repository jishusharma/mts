        Sources and rules

        You trade Deribit TESTNET; these are MAINNET public prices, testnet prints differ. Position, rungs and levels are saved in this browser only.

        Inverse contract: P&L BTC = size x (1/average - 1/mark), negated for a short. Distances = level - price on the candle venue; ATR15 = ATR14 of 15m bars.

        RSI14 Wilder, SMA14 of RSI, BB(20,2) population, ATR14 Wilder, day VWAP hlc3 from 00:00Z: SOURCED (your chart setup). WaveTrend 10/21/4, 0.015, lines 53/60, 200-bar warm-up: OURS (LazyBear defaults; your chart settings not yet known). Flag lines 30/70: OURS. Values include the forming bar. 4h = 1h bars folded on UTC 00/04/08/12/16/20Z (Deribit has no 4h candles).

        Funding: Deribit ticker funding_8h (accrues continuously), Bybit tickers.BTCUSDT fundingRate (paid every 8h at nextFundingTime). Positive = longs pay shorts. Mark - index = each venue's own premium. A year = rate x 3 x 365. All SOURCED (venue APIs); "about zero" under 0.001 % per 8h and "high" at 0.03 % or more (3x the 0.01 % interest baseline): OURS (STEPS.md). Bybit - Deribit last mixes USDT and USD, so it includes the USDT/USD rate.

        Analysts: CBP rows (analyst, publish day, bias, pointers) and the roster in CBP analysts.json, built into analyst_data.js by build_analyst_data.py (any session reading the chart reruns it; by hand: python MyTradingSys/tools/live_aid/build_analyst_data.py). Words and biases SOURCED (the analysts' videos); levels parsed by scripts/dev/analyst_levels.py; FRESH = newest row 3 days old or less, the 6 % band, AT 0.3 % and NEAR 1.5 %: OURS. NOT_FETCHED = an enabled analyst that ingestion skips (it fetches the "actionable" profile only). The store keeps 5 days of rows, so an older row reads NO ROW.

        News: Tree News public REST (news.treeofalpha.com/api/news), polled every 15 s while this page is open; each item carries its own time, source and importance score 0-10 (SOURCED). Score 7 or more is marked "high"; the glance counts high items in the last 60 min, EVENT at 3 or more (OURS, research/news_feeds_2026-10-30). Word tags hack / halt / etf / fed / war / delist / btc: OURS. On 2026-10-29 this feed had the Binance hack at 20:39:00 UTC, before the 20:40-20:45 fall. Context only.

        Liquidations: Bybit allLiquidation.BTCUSDT (price = bankruptcy price), Binance btcusdt@forceOrder (one print per second at most), Deribit trades carrying the liquidation flag. Side = the position that was liquidated (SOURCED, venue docs). 1/5/15 min sums since this page connected, "longs" or "shorts" when one side is at least 2x the other, big print = 100,000 USD or more: OURS (STEPS.md).

        What the money is doing: price vs open interest per venue (Deribit ticker open_interest in USD contracts, Bybit tickers openInterest and REST open-interest 5min in BTC, Binance fapi openInterest and openInterestHist in BTC: SOURCED). Each venue's change is in its own unit; "All venues" weights each venue's change by its open interest in BTC so a price move is not counted as new positions. Six sentences from the spec, flat under 0.1 %, venues disagree at +0.2 % vs -0.2 %, FLUSH = a 5m bar with its low 0.25 % or more under the previous close while Bybit OI falls 0.2 % or more (SQUEEZE mirrored), bars under 12 apart are one event, trapped line at OI +0.2 % since the event: OURS (research/eagle_eyes_picture_2026-10-28/OI_COMMENTARY_SPEC.md). MEASURED lines: C24 in BACKTEST_CANDIDATES.md. A feed older than 90 s reads NOT_IN_DATA.

        In-trade readings are descriptive, no advice. Rules and thresholds = OURS, fixed in STEPS.md before data was looked at. Data: Deribit mainnet public trades, book and open interest; candles from the strip venue. Rule R = 3 or more of the available flags against the side you typed; it names no size.
