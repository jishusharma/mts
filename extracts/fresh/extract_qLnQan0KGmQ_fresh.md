## VIDEO CONTEXT

Tom presents a crude oil swing-trade example and explains how to plan entries in high-volatility markets. The core sequence is: wait for price to return toward high-volume/fair-price areas, look for proof of buying or selling, use balance and acceptance as context, target the next high-volume area, and evaluate the risk-reward ratio before entering.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

1. The best trades occur where there is high volatility, especially for multi-day swing trades.
2. Markets tend to return to high-volume areas, which serve as fair-price zones.
3. Balance below previous balance after aggressive selling signals shifted supply and demand.
4. Absolute volume peaks produce notable market reactions and create target zones.
5. Proof is required before entry: excessive buying plus balance on a smaller timeframe.
6. The market is expected to move toward the last highest liquidity/high-volume area.
7. The final requirement is idea plus proof, then risk-reward ratio must be established and respected.
