## VIDEO CONTEXT

Tom gives an overview of how he and his team plan short-term trades. The process is not about fancy indicators. It connects dynamic context and order flow to get timing right. The workflow moves from macro trend structure to weekly profiles, intraday profiles, liquidity, order book, and footprint chart. He prepares several markets before the US session, then uses naked order books with remembered levels.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

1. The trading desk process has three core parts: interesting locations, balance/imbalance, and market speed.
2. Footprint charts, price acceptance, and market acceptance show when markets go out of balance or return to balance.
3. Weekly profiles reveal VPOC behavior, double distributions, single prints, and short squeeze risk.
4. Intraday profiles help establish business zones and support/resistance levels.
5. Liquidity behavior shows whether buying is stable or whether a quick move was only a lack of liquidity.
6. The order book combined with footprint traded volume shows what happened and what is happening now.
7. Preparation of multiple markets takes about 5 to 10 minutes per market before the session.
8. In naked order books, the trader waits for signals, absorption, and large orders to indicate probability.
