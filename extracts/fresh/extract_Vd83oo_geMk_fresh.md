## VIDEO CONTEXT

Tom challenges the promotion of heat maps as a liquidity tool. He says institutional traders and his team do not use heat maps because the order book is the true market. He shows why the heat map only shows where large orders were/are, but not how they were executed, who was aggressive, or whether liquidity will actually be taken. The video walks through order book, volume profile, footprint chart, delta, and market profiling as tools that provide better context.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

1. Heat map shows where large orders are and where large orders were, but not execution details.
2. Order books are described as the true market.
3. You need context to determine whether liquidity will be taken: market environment, session phase, and whether big or small players are active.
4. Footprint chart, delta, volume profile, and market profiling provide information the heat map lacks.
5. Trading in the direction of large orders, assuming they must be eaten, can produce repeated stop-outs and large drawdowns.
6. Tom's conclusion: heat maps can help identify levels, but are not a good way to build an edge by themselves.
