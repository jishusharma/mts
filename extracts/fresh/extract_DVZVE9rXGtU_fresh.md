## KEY FINDINGS

1. Day traders represent only about 2-3% of the entire market controlled by global money.
2. Large institutional pension funds may take several days to route orders.
3. Large order-flow footprints can reveal whether someone is building or closing a position.
4. Index trackers replicate an index by buying stocks according to their weighting; they cannot hold a DAX future for more than 7 years due to rolling.
5. Closing auctions often reveal whether institutions are done positioning; gap strategies can trade back to original value.
6. A huge order chasing the market usually means someone is in trouble, often due to a margin call.
7. Never stand in the way of a forced liquidation train; wait for the move to finish.
8. The oil negative price event and Bitcoin correction from 125,000 are used as examples of "nothing is impossible."
9. Commitment of Traders reports are useful context but not an edge by themselves.
10. Institutional investors need retail panic, fear, and greed to make markets volatile.
