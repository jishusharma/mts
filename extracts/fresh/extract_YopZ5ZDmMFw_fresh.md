## KEY FINDINGS

1. Tom presents a trading matrix sequence: chart, weekly profiles, intraday profiles, footprint/order flow, then order book.
2. A stable trend repeatedly has balance phases; a fast trend crashes down and tends to either continue or resolve upward as a V reversal.
3. Before trading a technical resistance, Tom looks left for technical obstacles.
4. Weekly profiles show whether the market is trending or in balance and what the current market environment is.
5. If price is in the middle of last week's range, the probability is high that balance continues; Tom does not look for impulsive moves there.
6. Previous week value area high/low helps identify whether price is inside or outside balance and whether to expect fast trend or sideways.
7. Intraday profiles: compare market opening to closing price; assess whether a gap makes logical sense.
8. If price dips at the open then rises into a new higher balance, gap-close speculators were wrong and supply/demand shifted higher.
9. Intraday profiles help locate next targets using naked POCs, old single print areas, and old value areas.
10. A complete profile may be repaired; then the question is whether price goes higher to repair everything or returns lower.
11. Footprint chart with bar delta, day delta, and accumulated volume shows price areas with no liquidity; market moves back toward old liquidity.
12. The market searches for supply and demand. Tom does not search for random breakout/counter setups without big-money context.
13. Order book analysis shows whether the market is aggressive/fast or lethargic/slow, and whether absorptions or large fake orders are present.
14. Trader DNA defines whether a trader prefers counter trades or breakout trades; setups should be specialized accordingly.
15. Trend following should be abandoned when momentum is no longer favorable; probability is high that price returns to fair price to find new buyers.
