## VIDEO SUMMARY

Tom Vorwald explains how to correctly use the Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) indicator. He identifies three distinct trading personalities and their corresponding VWAP applications: (1) trend-following traders use VWAP for pullbacks in trending markets, (2) counter-traders use VWAP as a profit target when price is extended, and (3) scalpers use VWAP in sideways markets to trade oscillations. Tom emphasizes that VWAP must always be used with context — specifically, whether the market is trending (~30% of time) or balanced (~70%). Using VWAP without context leads to excessive stop losses.
