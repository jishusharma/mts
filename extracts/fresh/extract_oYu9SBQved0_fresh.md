## KEY FINDINGS

1. Trading requires mental strength, discipline, and routine; trading is a business, not a casino.
2. Most traders do not keep a journal; journaling is a major reason for failure.
3. Indicators alone cannot make money consistently; a programmer backtested all indicators and found nothing that works consistently.
4. Traders need to identify their trader DNA and choose strategies matching their personality and patience.
5. Stop-losses are insurance, not mistakes, and should be accepted as part of the process.
6. Distraction is a major problem; a dedicated trading PC with no unrelated software is recommended.
7. Bad days happen; do not take frustration out on family, and use sport, meditation, or journal review to reset.
8. Reward yourself for discipline, not only for profits.
9. Retail traders often buy at hype and sell at panic; the opposite behavior is needed.
10. Consistency and deep understanding of what one is doing make trading feel better.
