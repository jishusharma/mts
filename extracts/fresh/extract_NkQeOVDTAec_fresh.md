## VIDEO SUMMARY

Tom Vorwald explains the foundational logic of volume profile trading. The core concept is the "IDEA + PROOF" framework: first identify a price area where major participants have traded significant volume (the idea), then wait for a setup (proof) that the market is reacting at that level. Tom emphasizes that volume profile alone is not a strategy — it is a tool for locating zones where setups become meaningful. He introduces the concept of planning from one volume node to the next, calculating risk-reward ratios, and tracking key figures (hit rate, average R:R) across 200-300 trades.
