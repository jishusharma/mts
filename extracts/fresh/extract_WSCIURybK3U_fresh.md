## VIDEO CONTEXT

Tom opens with the claim that 99% of retail traders lose money, and states:
> "90% of traders loses 90% of their money in 90 days. Why? Because they have no clear risk management."

He says the typical approach — "I choose 1% or 2% of my capital from my risk" — is not good and is boring. The video focuses on how he builds his structure to know when he is allowed to trade, which products he can trade, and what key figures matter.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

1. Reference values are mandatory before any trade: stop loss and predefined target.
2. Risk-reward ratio must be calculable from entry, stop, and target.
3. Max drawdown is the controlling variable for position size, not simply a fixed 1% or 2% risk.
4. He states a hard rule: never allow more than a 20% drawdown of trading capital.
5. Even 1% risk per trade can be too much if the strategy has long losing streaks.
6. Product knowledge matters: contract tick values and fees determine whether a trade is even allowed.
7. Traders must account for cost of living and taxes before judging whether daily points are enough to build wealth.
