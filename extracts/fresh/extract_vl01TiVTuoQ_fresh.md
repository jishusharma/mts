## VIDEO SUMMARY

Tom introduces order flow, footprint charts, and the order book. He explains:

1. Order flow shows who is buying, who is selling, and how aggressively.
2. Footprint charts show exact buys and sells at a particular time.
3. The order book shows limit orders on the bid and ask sides.
4. Large orders indicate where liquidity sits, and markets tend to move toward liquidity.
5. Traders must know normal order sizes for the specific product.
6. Price areas with low past volume are thin areas where fast movement and rejection can occur.
7. Large orders are usually real and likely to be filled, though spoofing exists.
8. After a large order fills, the next large order can provide further direction.
9. Watching whether large orders remain or are pulled gives information.
10. Combining order book, volume profile, speed, and large orders gives an edge over normal charts.
