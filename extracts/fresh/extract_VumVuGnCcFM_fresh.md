## VIDEO SUMMARY

Tom explains VWAP as a session-based volume-weighted moving average. He emphasizes that many traders and algorithms use VWAP mean-reversion incorrectly, especially in trending markets. Key points:

1. VWAP is reset at the start of each session.
2. VWAP is useful for identifying balance and possible bounces in balanced markets.
3. In trending markets, the market often does not return to VWAP immediately.
4. Single prints, support/resistance, opening phase, profile shapes, and news help determine whether the market is trending.
5. The market returns to equilibrium roughly 70% of the time, but true trend days occur about 20% of the time and can close at the day's low.
6. Tom warns against pyramiding and continuously building positions in counter-trend VWAP strategies.
7. In strong trends, traders may instead look for trades at VWAP in the direction of the trend.
