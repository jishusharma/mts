## VIDEO SUMMARY

Tom Vorwald provides a deeper explanation of volume profile mechanics: how volume is aggregated at price levels, the concepts of balance (fair price) vs imbalance (unfair price), the definitions of Point of Control (POC), Value Area High (VAH), Value Area Low (VAL), and Low Volume Nodes (LVN). He explains how markets tend to return to fair price areas (~70% of phases) and how traders can use these zones for breakout, counter-trade, and trend-continuation strategies. Tom emphasizes that volume profile should not be used in isolation.
