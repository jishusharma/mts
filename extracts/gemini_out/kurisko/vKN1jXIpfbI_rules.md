## Notes (OURS)
In this foundational video, John Kurisko ("DayTraderRockStar") outlines the structural charting basis of his methodology:
1. **Trend & Channels:** Connecting minimum 2 swing pivot points defines trendlines; parallel bounds define a trading channel. Each subsequent test of the channel boundary provides a high-probability reaction opportunity.
2. **Moving Averages:** 20, 50, and 200 EMAs serve as mean-reversion benchmarks and dynamic support/resistance zones. Prices overextended from the 20 EMA tend to revert to the mean.
3. **Horizontal Levels:** Support and resistance levels require at least two historical pivot tests to be confirmed as valid reaction levels for 3rd and 4th touch entries.
4. **Execution Boundaries:** No formal stop-loss calculation, numerical target, indicator filter, or position sizing formula is provided in this lesson.
