## RULE 13: FTA DOES NOT PREVENT SWING TRADING — IT ENABLES IT

> "taking profit at FTA means it's impossible to swing trade which is a very common criticism that I've heard... I'd say the opposite is true and that argument is based on a misconception"
> — Yp0k8HD5xro, ~37:30

> "the base function or on the base layer we move from weekly to weekly from daily to daily from early to hourly etc etc but we use interim or levels in between those high timeframe structures to inform our decision-making"
> — Yp0k8HD5xro, ~38:00

**Context:** A common criticism is that FTA prevents catching big moves. The opposite is true — FTA is a framework for swing trading. Entry, target, and stop are based on high timeframe levels (weekly-to-weekly, daily-to-daily). Interim lower timeframe levels are used to inform management decisions, not to prematurely exit. Without this framework, swing traders either do nothing (set-and-forget) or over-manage on noise.
