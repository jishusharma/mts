## RULE 2: BREAKOUT VS RETEST = RISK/REWARD TRADE-OFF
> "less certainty often offers superior risk reward... more certainty often offers inferior risk reward... if you're buying the retest of the level it's not clear whether the breakout's going to hold... but you get the premium if you will or the benefit of superior risk reward whereas if you're waiting for all the bullishness to kick in... the market will necessarily be further away from the breakout level"
> — inqHN8q57X4, ~09:30

> "if you wait for a re-test you get superior risk reward but no trade guarantee... if you trade the breakout you get a guaranteed trade in most cases but at the cost of an inferior risk to reward"
> — inqHN8q57X4, ~10:30

**Context:** Breakout entry = guaranteed fill but worse R:R. Retest entry = better R:R but no guarantee price returns.

## RULE 4: IMMEDIATE VS ROUNDED RETEST

> — inqHN8q57X4, ~16:00

> "the main distinguishing factor is time and space if you're trading here there's very little time and space between the break of the level and where the market is now right you're trading the breakout itself whereas here more time has passed a lot has happened and you're trading a return to the breakup point"
> — inqHN8q57X4, ~17:30

> "rounded re-tests tend to be clearer setups so the fact that the re-test is rounded implies that a meaningful break took place so you essentially eradicate the risk of falling for a failed breakout"
> — inqHN8q57X4, ~18:30

**Context:** Rounded retests (time/space since break) are clearer setups than immediate retests. Time/space validates the break.
