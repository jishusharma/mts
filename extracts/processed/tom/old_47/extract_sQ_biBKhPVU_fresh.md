## VIDEO CONTEXT

Tom introduces three indicators he says "you must have in 2026." The focus is on understanding where global money is, where imbalance exists, and how to use simpler information sources rather than complex smart-money concepts. The three indicators are presented in sequence: major public newspapers as a counter indicator, volume profile composite, and average volume/volume spikes.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

1. Major public newspapers, especially when publishing fear or greed stories, are treated as a counter indicator.
2. Geopolitical news is considered short-lived: "political stock markets have short legs."
3. Long-term investment buying may be considered when mainstream magazines portray stocks as extremely dangerous.
4. Volume profile composite should be built from the last major high to the last major low, not four or five years of data.
5. Low volume areas are areas of rejection and unfair prices; the market runs back to liquidity and fair prices.
6. High volume areas are where larger participants build and wind down positions; the market returns to these areas until a new high volume area forms.
7. Exponential volume spikes indicate exhaustion, often followed by reversal or sideways movement.
8. Extremes in indicators such as the Fear and Greed Index should not be assumed to tip immediately; a reversal signal or sideways phase is required.
9. Tom directs traders to supply and demand basics rather than complex indicators.
