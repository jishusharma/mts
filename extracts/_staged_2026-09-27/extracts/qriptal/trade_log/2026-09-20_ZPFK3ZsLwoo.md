## 5. DECISION RULES / OBSERVATIONS

1. **[GENERAL-RULE]** Wickless candles act as magnets that price returns to and reverses from, on any asset/timeframe.
   - Quote: *"Wickless candles, take note of them. They don't just work on Bitcoin, take note of them on every single asset. You have a wickless candle, you jump into it, you get the recovery to the upside."* [16:08]-[16:28] (also stated for BTC at [7:39]-[7:44])
   - Restatement: A wickless candle marks a level price tends to revisit and reverse from, regardless of asset or timeframe.

2. **[SITUATIONAL]** On a specific short, he placed his stop at the daily open because reaching it would signal strength.
   - Quote: *"I put my stop loss right at the daily open because in my opinion reaching it would be a sign of strength."* [6:01]-[6:07]
   - Restatement: For that trade, a return to the daily open was treated as invalidating the short thesis.

3. **[GENERAL-RULE]** Small-bodied, high-volume candles ("imbalances") mark weak/bad positions that are very likely, though not certain, to be revisited.
   - Quote: *"small body candles, volume anomalies, it is very unlikely that we leave that liquidity behind... it is just very very very unlikely."* [27:52]-[28:03]
   - Restatement: High volume paired with a small candle body indicates poorly-positioned traders whose liquidity price is likely to return for, though he stresses this is not a 100% rule.

4. **[GENERAL-RULE]** Market regime changes which side liquidity favors: bull markets forgive longs, bear markets forgive shorts.
   - Quote: *"a bull market is more forgiving to the longs and the bare market is more forgiving to the shorts."* [50:40]-[50:49]
   - Restatement: In his framework, liquidity below price is not always taken in a bull market because aggressive longs are structurally favored, and the reverse holds in a bear market.

5. **[GENERAL-RULE]** Whales place limit orders at round psychological price levels rather than precise technical levels.
   - Quote: *"Whales mainly use limit orders because they're not super accurate chartists like me... they don't come in the chart and say, 'Oh, I have a level at 75,178.'... If that's why if you see someone give you such an accurate level, in my humble opinion, dismiss this guy as an analyst."* [33:32]-[35:07]
   - Restatement: Large orders cluster at round psychological prices; an analyst citing an oddly precise price level is, in his view, not credible.

6. **[GENERAL-RULE]** Price direction reflects whale limit-order fills against retail, opposite to the naive reading of candle color.
   - Quote: *"Price is moving down because whales are bidding. They're buying. The people that are selling are the retail traders... [on the way up] they have limit orders to sell what they bought."* [33:36]-[35:52]
   - Restatement: A falling price means whale buy-limit orders are filling against retail selling; a rising price means whale sell-limit orders are filling against retail buying.

7. **[GENERAL-RULE]** Avoid shorting an asset with no defined range/structure yet; wait for a range and a deviation first.
   - Quote: *"when I see a coin like this I would avoid shorts until can form some sort of a structure... before looking for a confident short position, I would like for a range to develop."* [20:31]-[21:03]
   - Restatement: He requires a defined trading range and a sweep/deviation of it before treating a short setup as valid.

8. **[GENERAL-RULE]** No setup is ever treated as a certainty.
   - Quote: *"I'm not going to say that this is 100% setup because 100% don't doesn't exist in the markets."* [13:49]-[13:55]
   - Restatement: Even his highest-confluence setups are stated with an explicit caveat that they can fail.

9. **[SITUATIONAL]** Current market classified as a local range within a broader new bull market, which is why he expects a nearby imbalance cluster to get recovered.
